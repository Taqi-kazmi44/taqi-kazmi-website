Qanoon ko waqt se pehle samajhna, masle ke baad nahi

Main Taqi Kazmi hun, Karachi mein LLB student. Mera maqsad Pakistan ka qanoon aam aadmi tak aisi zubaan mein pahunchana hai jo har koi samajh sake. Main is website par apne aap ko lawyer, advocate ya law firm ke tor par represent nahi karta.

Aksar log qanoon se tab milte hain jab kuch ghalat ho chuka hota hai. Main is soch ko badalna chahta hun. Isi liye main qanooni maloomat share karta hun: asal qanoon kya kehta hai, procedure kya hai, aam ghaltiyan kya hain, aur aap ke huquq kya hain.

Kya kaam karta hun

  • Legal news: Pakistan ki adalaton aur qanoon sazi ki verified khabrein, official references ke sath.
  • Judgment citations: Supreme Court aur High Court faislon ki source-linked citations (2026 SCMR series).
  • PPC series: Pakistan Penal Code ki sections strict legal order mein, har section ka apna visual card.
  • Know Your Rights guides: FIR, khula, succession certificate, tenancy aur rozmarra qanooni huquq par practical guides.
  • Documentation & registration assistance: Company, NGO, trust, foundation registrations aur agreement drafting mein madad.

Research ka usool

Meri research ka usool sakht hai: har record ke sath available source ya exact citation di jati hai. Jahan primary document, fee, processing time ya document list confirm na ho, us ka status wazeh tor par bataya jata hai.

Professional scope: Registration, documentation aur legal information assistance Pakistan bhar mein available hai. Karachi primary base hai; Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar, Quetta, Hyderabad aur dusre shehron ke clients ke liye federal aur online guidance available hai.

Legal service provider ka intikhab

Legal service provider ka intikhab sirf marketing claim par nahi hona chahiye. Relevant practice area, bar enrollment, shehri jurisdiction, documented experience aur clear fee scope verify karein. Is website par legal documentation, registrations aur procedural information ki services di gayi hain.
