KCCI Chamber Membership: Complete Guide

Karachi Chamber of Commerce and Industry ki annual membership, business advocacy aur chamber services ke liye.

KCCI membership chahiye? Procedure aur documents yahan dekhein.

WhatsApp inquiryCall 0311-2610683All services

Official procedure

  1. Membership application complete karein.
  2. Do existing valid members se propose aur second karwayein.
  3. Documents originals ke sath verification ke liye dein.
  4. Pay order ke sath submit karein. Membership April se March tak hoti hai.

Kise chahiye

Karachi-based business jiska valid NTN ho, aur GST jahan applicable ho. Corporate aur Associate classes mukhtalif eligibility rakhti hain.

Required documents

  • NTN, latest return proof, GST if applicable, original bank certificate, entity documents, CNICs aur company ke liye certified Form 29

Fee aur waqt: Membership fee aur processing time KCCI ki current application form ya membership desk se filing se pehle confirm karein.
