Tenant Verification Online in Sindh

Complete tenant verification through the official Sindh Police Tenant Registration System and confirm the current document list on the portal.

What the process involves

  1. Prepare landlord, tenant and property particulars
  2. Use the official Sindh Police Tenant Registration System
  3. Confirm current uploads and any fee on the portal
  4. Keep the acknowledgment or verification record with the tenancy file
