Mailsi: Seven-Year-Old Girl Allegedly Raped and Murdered, Body Found in a Sack

A seven-year-old girl went missing from her home. Five days later, her body was found stuffed in a sack.

The girl, identified in the report only as 'D', had gone missing from her home at Mauza Chatiana, in the Mailsi Saddar area, about five days ago. On Tuesday night, during a search operation, a police team spotted a suspect, identified only as 'T', carrying a sack on his back and trying to move it to another place. On seeing the police, he dropped the sack containing the girl's body and tried to flee.

The police recovered the girl's body and chased the suspect. According to the police, he shot himself with his pistol, fearing arrest. Both bodies were shifted to hospital for post-mortem examinations. According to the police team, the suspect had allegedly raped and strangled the girl before stuffing her body into the sack.

What the law says: rape is punishable with death or imprisonment for life under Section 376 of the Pakistan Penal Code, and murder is punishable with death or imprisonment for life under Section 302. The sections of the FIR have not been reported in the press, so no FIR sections are stated here. Because the suspect is dead, this child will never get a trial, a court finding, or a sentence passed in her case. The police version of how the suspect died has not been tested in a court. The allegations against the suspect are yet to be proved in court.

What this means for you: if a child in your family goes missing, report it at once to the nearest police station or call police helpline 15, insist on an FIR and a medical examination, and keep a copy of both. If the police refuse to register an FIR, complain to the District Police Officer of your district, or approach the Justice of the Peace under Section 22-A of the Code of Criminal Procedure for registration of the case. For a child in danger, the Child Protection and Welfare Bureau helpline is 1121.
