Karachi Dv Zero Convictions

Over 1,000 domestic violence complaints every month in Karachi. Zero convictions in six months. The law is in the books. Justice is not on the ground.

Over 1,000 domestic violence complaints every month in Karachi. Zero convictions in six months. The law is in the books. Justice is not on the ground.

SSDO's RTI data, reported by the Tribune: in Sindh from January to June 2025, 204 cases were registered, 98 challans were submitted, 70 cases went under trial, 14 were withdrawn or ended in acquittal, and no one was convicted. The HRCP reports that 165 women were killed in Sindh in 2024.

For law students: Sindh's Domestic Violence (Prevention and Protection) Act, 2013 is not just a route to an FIR. Section 9 provides for applying for a protection order, Section 11 covers the protection order (against violence, contact, and eviction from the home) and the residence order, Section 12 provides monetary relief, and Section 21 provides the Protection Officer. The real causes of the conviction gap are withdrawing cases under family pressure, weak investigation, and hesitation to report before male officers. This is the most important case study in gender justice.

For affected women: do not rely on the FIR alone. Also obtain a protection order from the court. Help is available on 1099 and 1094.
