Double Taxation Dispute: NTC Must Decide in Two Weeks

Can two provinces tax the same service? The Lahore High Court has told the National Tax Council to settle the Punjab vs Sindh sales tax dispute in two weeks.

Can two provinces tax the same service? That question has now forced the National Tax Council to act.

The Lahore High Court has directed the National Tax Council to decide, within two weeks, whether sales tax on stock trading services belongs to Punjab or Sindh. The order came on a petition filed by Zafar Securities and others, and was reported by Dawn on 25 September 2026.

The facts: the brokerage firms are based in Lahore, with employees and customers in Punjab, and they have been paying sales tax to the Punjab Revenue Authority. But the Sindh Revenue Board also demanded tax on the same services, arguing that trading happens through the Pakistan Stock Exchange's central system in Karachi.

The legal point: the same service cannot be taxed twice by two provinces. The petitioners have asked that if the tax is found payable to Sindh, the amount already paid to Punjab be transferred, with no second demand, penalty or additional liability. Their application before the NTC, filed on 5 December 2025, had remained pending for over nine months.

Aam aadmi ke liye iska matlab: agar aap shares khareedte ya bechte hain to broker ki service par tax sirf ek dafa lag sakta hai, do soobon ko do dafa nahi.
