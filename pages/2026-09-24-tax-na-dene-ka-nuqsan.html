Tax return file na karne ke nuqsanat: rozana penalty se bank attach tak

Tax return file na karna ab mehenga par sakta hai. Income Tax Ordinance, 2001 ke tehat nuqsanat: rozana penalty, bank attach, double withholding, surcharge.

Tax return file na karna ab mehenga par sakta hai.

Income Tax Ordinance, 2001 ke tehat nuqsanat:

1. ROZANA PENALTY: Notice ke baad late filing par Rs1,000 rozana ya tax ka 0.1% rozana, jo zyada ho. Minimum Rs10,000 (Section 182).

2. BANK ATTACH: FBR aapke bank account se raqam recover kar sakta hai (Section 140).

3. DOUBLE WITHHOLDING: ATL mein naam na ho to har transaction par zyada tax kat-ta hai (Section 100BA).

4. SURCHARGE: Deadline ke baad ATL mein wapas aane ke liye surcharge dena hoga.

Kya aap ne is saal apna return file kiya? Comment mein batayein.
