Fresh CNIC ka tareeqa

  1. Nearest NADRA Registration Centre ka route confirm karein aur token hasil karein.
  2. Photograph, fingerprints aur signature capture complete karein.
  3. Printed application form ki tamam entries review karein.
  4. Attestation ya eligible blood relative biometric path official staff se confirm karein.
  5. NADRA ke mutabiq fresh ya new CNIC website se apply nahi hota, is liye centre visit zaroori hai.
  6. Renewal ke liye NADRA Registration Centre ya Pak Identity Mobile App ka eligible route use karein.
  7. Current fee aur category official channel par confirm karein.
