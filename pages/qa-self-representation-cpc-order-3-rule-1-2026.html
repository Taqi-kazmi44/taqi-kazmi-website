Can You Fight Your Own Case Without a Lawyer in Pakistan?

One of the most common questions people ask: must you hire a lawyer to fight a case, or can you appear in court yourself? The short answer is yes, you can appear in person. The Code of Civil Procedure, 1908, says so in plain words, and the criminal law and the Constitution protect your right to choose your own lawyer if you do want one. This page states the rule exactly as the law does.

What Order III, Rule 1 of the CPC says

Order III, Rule 1 of the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908, provides that any appearance, application or act in or to any Court, required or authorised by law to be made or done by a party in such Court, may, except where otherwise expressly provided by any law for the time being in force, be made or done by the party in person, or by his recognised agent, or by a pleader appearing, applying or acting, as the case may be, on his behalf. The rule adds one proviso: any such appearance shall, if the Court so directs, be made by the party in person.

The criminal side: Section 340(1) CrPC and Article 10(1) of the Constitution

In a criminal case, Section 340(1) of the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898, provides that any person accused of an offence before a Criminal Court, or against whom proceedings are instituted under the Code in any such Court, may of right be defended by a pleader. Article 10(1) of the Constitution of Pakistan, 1973, protects the same right: no person who is arrested shall be denied the right to consult, and to be defended by, a legal practitioner of his choice. Both provisions give you a right to a lawyer, they do not compel you to engage one.

The limits you should know

The right to appear in person is not unlimited. The court can direct that you appear in person. In some higher courts, a party may need the court's permission to argue in person. Court procedure, the law of evidence and cross examination are technical, and a mistake can cost you the case, so in a serious or complex matter a lawyer is strongly advisable even though the law allows you to appear yourself.

The key legal point

  1. You can appear in court and argue your own case. No law forces you to hire a lawyer.
  2. In a civil case the right comes from Order III, Rule 1 of the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908. In a criminal case, Section 340(1) CrPC and Article 10(1) of the Constitution protect your right to be defended by a pleader of your choice.
