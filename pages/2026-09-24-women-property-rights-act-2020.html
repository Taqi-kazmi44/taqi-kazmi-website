Khwateen ka jaaidad ka haq: 60 din mein insaaf ka rasta

Enforcement of Women's Property Rights Act 2020 ke tehet Ombudsperson jaaidad se mehrum khawateen ki complaint par karwai karta hai. Complaint ke 60 din ke andar faisle ko tarjeeh di jati hai.

Jaaidad se mehrum khawateen ko civil court mein saalon intezar ki zaroorat nahi. 60 din ka rasta mojood hai jise aksar log jante hi nahi.

Enforcement of Women's Property Rights Act 2020 ke tehet Ombudsperson kisi bhi aisi khawateen ki complaint par karwai kar sakta hai jise uski jaaidad se mehrum kiya gaya ho. Tariqa seedha hai: Ombudsperson muamla Deputy Commissioner ko bhejta hai jo 15 din mein inquiry karke report deta hai. Complaint ke 60 din ke andar faisle ko tarjeeh di jati hai. Phir police aur district administration uska qabza aur title wapas dilane ke paband hain, aur koi adaalat Ombudsperson ke orders ke khilaf stay nahi de sakti.

Agar khawateen khud complaint na kar sake to koi bhi shakhs ya NGO uski taraf se complaint kar sakta hai. Senate ne ye bill 14 February 2020 ko manzoor kiya tha.
