Can Talaq Be Effective by Just Saying It?

One of the most common family-law questions: does saying "talaq" once end the marriage? The law of Pakistan says no. The written notice to the Union Council is a mandatory legal step, and skipping it is a punishable offence.

The legal rule: Section 7 of the Muslim Family Laws Ordinance, 1961

Section 7(1) says that any man who wishes to divorce his wife shall, as soon as may be after the pronouncement of talaq in any form whatsoever, give the Chairman of the Union Council a notice in writing of his having done so, and shall supply a copy thereof to the wife. So the law does not ask how many times talaq was said; it asks whether the written notice reached the Union Council.

Section 7(3) then fixes the timeline: a talaq, unless revoked earlier expressly or otherwise, shall not be effective until the expiration of ninety days from the day on which the notice is delivered to the Chairman. During these 90 days the divorce can still be revoked.

Reconciliation: the Arbitration Council

Within thirty days of receiving the notice, the Chairman must constitute an Arbitration Council for the purpose of bringing about a reconciliation between the parties, and the Council must take all steps necessary to bring about such reconciliation (Section 7(4)). The law therefore gives the couple a formal chance to save the marriage before the divorce becomes final.

Skipping the notice is a crime

Section 7(2) makes it an offence to contravene the notice requirement: whoever fails to send the written notice to the Chairman is punishable with simple imprisonment for a term which may extend to one year, or with fine which may extend to five thousand rupees, or with both. Merely saying talaq and walking away, without sending the notice, can land the husband in jail.

The key legal point

  1. What this means for you: if talaq was only spoken and no written notice was ever sent to the Union Council, the legal divorce process is incomplete, and the wife's rights, including maintenance, remain protected. The effective-divorce certificate is issued only after the 90-day period following a proper notice.
