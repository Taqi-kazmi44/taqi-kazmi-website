Sector E-12 plot dispute: sirf agreement malkiyat sabit nahi karta

Plot sirf stamp paper ke agreement par khareeda hai? April 2026 mein Supreme Court ne ek 26 saal purane dawe ko mustarad kar ke khabardar kar diya: sirf agreement malkiyat sabit nahi karta, gawah pesh karne honge.

Plot sirf stamp paper ke agreement par khareeda hai? April 2026 mein Supreme Court ne ek 26 saal purane dawe ko mustarad kar ke sab ko khabardar kar diya: sirf agreement malkiyat sabit nahi karta.

QANOON KYA KEHTA HAI: Sector E-12 Islamabad ke plot ke tanaze mein Supreme Court (Chief Justice Yahya Afridi aur Justice Shahid Bilal Hassan, faisla Justice Shahid Bilal Hassan ne tehreer kiya) ne Islamabad High Court ka faisla khatam kar diya. Adaalat ne qaraar diya ke qanoon e shahadat ke tehat agreement ki bunyad par malkiyat ka dawa karne wale ko agreement ke gawahon ko adaalat mein pesh karna hoga. Gawah pesh na karne ki surat mein dawedar ke khilaf adverse inference liya jayega, yani adaalat samjhegi ke gawah pesh na karna dawedar ke nuqsan mein hai. Sath hi adaalat ne kaha ke CDA ke record mein darj third-party rights ko nazar andaz nahi kiya ja sakta, adaalat ko sirf zahiri qanuni pehlu nahi, tamam haqaiq dekhne honge.

AAM GHALTI: Pakistan mein lakhon log plot aur makaan sirf stamp paper par lete dete hain, baghair gawahon aur proper title transfer ke. Ye faisla kehta hai ke adaalat mein aisa agreement akela aap ko malik sabit nahi karega. Khareed se pehle title verify karwayein, gawah rakhein, aur transfer ka proper record banwayein.
