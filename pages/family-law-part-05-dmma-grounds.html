Family Law Part 5: Tanseekh-e-Nikah (DMMA 1939)

Pakistan mein bohat si khawateen nahi jantin ke qanoon unhein adaalat ke zariye shaadi khatam karne ki 9 makhsoos wajuhat deta hai. Ye mukammal qanooni fehrist hai, aam zuban mein samjhai gayi.

FAMILY LAW SERIES | Hissa 5 az 10

Pakistan mein bohat si khawateen nahi jantin ke qanoon unhein adaalat ke zariye shaadi khatam karne ki 9 makhsoos wajuhat deta hai. Ye mukammal qanooni fehrist hai, aam zuban mein samjhai gayi.

QANOON KYA KEHTA HAI: Dissolution of Muslim Marriages Act 1939 ki Section 2 ke tehat Muslim qanoon ke mutabiq shaadi shuda aurat in wajuhat par dissolution ki decree le sakti hai: (1) shohar ka pata 4 saal se maloom na ho; (2) 2 saal se kharcha na diya ho; (2A) shohar ne Muslim Family Laws Ordinance 1961 ki khilaf warzi kar ke doosri shaadi ki ho; (3) shohar 7 saal ya zyada qaid ho, saza final honi chahiye; (4) 3 saal se baghair jayez wajah azdawaji faraiz ada na kiye hon; (5) shaadi ke waqt na-mard ho aur ab tak ho; (6) 2 saal se deewana ho ya khatarnak jinsi beemari ho; (7) option of puberty, yani sarparast ne 15 saal se pehle shaadi ki ho aur larki 18 saal se pehle, hambistari se pehle, shaadi se inkar kar de; (8) zulm, jis mein aadatana maar peet, buri auratyon se talluq, ghair ikhlaqi zindagi par majboor karna, us ki jaidad bech dena, mazhabi ibadat mein rukawat, ya biwiyon mein na-insafi shamil hai; (9) Muslim qanoon ke tehat koi bhi aur jayez wajah.

PROCEDURE: Biwi Family Court mein dissolution ka dawa dayer karti hai, apni wajah saboot se sabit karti hai, aur adaalat decree deti hai. Lapata shohar wali wajah par decree 6 mah baad effective hoti hai taake use hazir hone ka mauqa mile.

AAM GHALTI: Samajhna ke aurton ke liye sirf khula ka rasta hai. 1939 ke Act ki statutory wajuhat aksar mazboot rasta hain, khas taur par jahan shohar qasoorwar ho, kyunke ye biwi ka mehr mehfooz rakhti hain.

Hawala: Dissolution of Muslim Marriages Act 1939, Section 2. Mukammal text: https://www.courtkutchehry.com/bare-acts/dissolution-of-muslim-marriages-act-1939/
