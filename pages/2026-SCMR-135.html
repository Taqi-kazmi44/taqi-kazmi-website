2026 SCMR 135

A raid was conducted in Karachi, but no video of the spot was ever made.
The Supreme Court put the raid itself under question.

In Abdul Mateen Mehboob vs The State, 2026 SCMR 135, decided at the Karachi Registry, the recovery of a psychotropic substance (Rivotril/Clonazepam) was made without any videography or photography. The accused was neither the exporter, nor the shipper, nor the clearing agent, nor the financer, and he had no knowledge of the drugs at all. Since his role was the same as that of a co-accused already on bail, the rule of "like cases should be treated alike" applied, and bail was granted. The Court reminded everyone: the purpose of criminal law is to make the accused face trial, not to punish him before trial.

For law students, the concepts to master are the rule of consistency and parity in bail, further inquiry under Section 497(2) CrPC, and the importance of videography in raids. For the people of Karachi, the message is clear: procedural violations in raids will no longer survive in court.

Case: Abdul Mateen Mehboob vs The State, 2026 SCMR 135. Supreme Court of Pakistan, decided 24 July 2025. Bench: Muhammad Ali Mazhar, Syed Hasan Azhar Rizvi and Aqeel Ahmed Abbasi, JJ.
