PLD 2026 SC 238: Courts Must Hear the Child in Custody Cases

Supreme Court of Pakistan (two-member bench: Justice Syed Mansoor Ali Shah and Justice Aqeel Ahmed Abbasi) held in Dr Muhammad Asif vs Dr Sana Sattar and others, PLD 2026 SC 238, decided May 2025 (C.R.P. No. 458 of 2024 in C.P.L.A. No. 2514 of 2024), that hearing and respecting the voice of the child in custody and guardianship cases is a binding obligation under the UN Convention on the Rights of the Child. The Supreme Court of Pakistan has ruled in PLD 2026 SC 238 that courts, particularly family courts, must hear and respect the voice of the child in custody and guardianship cases. The two-member bench of Justice Syed Mansoor Ali Shah and Justice Aqeel Ahmed Abbasi decided Dr Muhammad Asif vs Dr Sana Sattar and others (C.R.P. No. 458 of 2024 in C.P.L.A. No. 2514 of 2024; judgment authored by Justice Shah) in May 2025.

The Court held that hearing the child is not an aspirational goal but a binding obligation under the UN Convention on the Rights of the Child. A child of sufficient age and maturity must be given a meaningful opportunity to express views on custody, and those views must receive due consideration. The best interests of the child are the paramount consideration, and welfare extends far beyond financial or physical well-being to emotional, psychological, educational, cultural and developmental needs.

Dismissing the review petition, the Court directed the parties to strictly adhere to the visitation schedule determined by the lower appellate court; custody continues with the mother unless and until the father moves a fresh application before a competent forum on a material change in circumstances.
