Inheritance Law (5/7): Walidain ka virasat mein muqarar hissa

Surah An-Nisa (4:11) ke mutabiq agar marhoom ki aulad ho to maa aur baap mein se har ek ko 1/6 milega. Walidain ka hissa un ka zaati haq hai.

INHERITANCE LAW SERIES | Hissa 5 az 7

Bete ke inteqal ke baad dada dadi ko buzurg hain, unhein kya chahiye keh kar hissa na dena qanoon ki nazar mein zulm hai. Walidain ka hissa bhi Quran ne muqarar kiya hai.

QANOON KYA KEHTA HAI: Surah An-Nisa (4:11) ke mutabiq agar marhoom ki aulad ho to us ke maa aur baap mein se har ek ko kul jaidad ka chhata hissa (1/6) milega. Agar marhoom ki aulad na ho to maa ko teesra hissa (1/3) milega, lekin agar marhoom ke bhai behan hon to maa ka hissa ghat kar chhata (1/6) ho jayega. Aulad na hone ki surat mein baap baqi bachi hui jaidad ka waris banta hai. Ghor karein: maa baap dono ko 1/6 milne ka usool sirf us waqt hai jab marhoom ki aulad ho, warna hissay mukhtalif hain.

AAM GHALTI: Pota poti ki parwarish ke naam par dada dadi ka hissa kha jana. Yaad rakhein, walidain ka hissa un ka zaati haq hai, aur is se dastbardar hone ka faisla sirf wo khud apni marzi se kar sakte hain, koi aur un ki taraf se nahi.

Hawala: Surah An-Nisa (4:11)
