71 Years Later, Sisters Get Their Inheritance Back: Supreme Court

Supreme Court restores female heirs' inheritance after 71 years: the 1955 mutation and all later transactions declared illegal and void. Noor Muhammad vs Ghulam Haider, reported 1 July 2026.

In 1955, two brothers took their sisters' share of the family property, claiming their father had orally gifted it to them. 71 years later, the Supreme Court has given it back.

A two-member bench of Justice Shahid Bilal Hassan and Justice Shakeel Ahmad set aside the trial court, the appellate court and the Lahore High Court, all of which had upheld the alleged oral gift. The Court declared the 1955 revenue mutation and every transaction built on it illegal and void, and ordered the revenue record corrected.

The ruling is clear: inheritance is not a favour from male relatives. It is a legal and Sharia right that vests in every heir the moment the owner dies. Anyone claiming an oral gift must prove it with independent evidence. A mutation entry or long possession proves nothing.
