Application ka tareeqa

  1. Official NADRA succession portal kholein aur apna case type select karein.
  2. Sindh ke liye current eligibility aur competent route confirm karein.
  3. Portal ya facilitation unit se current prescribed documents verify karein.
  4. Tamaam legal heirs ki correct identity aur relationship record tayyar rakhein.
  5. Estate ki moveable aur immovable property details organize karein.
  6. Authorization, affidavit, fee aur biometric requirements official authority se current basis par confirm karein.
  7. Submission receipt aur reference number sambhal kar rakhein aur status track karein.
