  • Supreme Court Judgment Summaries 2026: The Wife Keeps the Choice Between Gold or Cash Dower (13 Oct 2026)
    Where a family court decree affords alternate modes of satisfying dower (seven tolas of gold or Rs.100,000), the decree-holder wife retains the liberty to choose; where the gold cannot be returned, the executing court may direct payment of its current market value. Amin-ud-Din Khan and Syed Hasan Azhar Rizvi, JJ, Shafqat Ali vs Mst. Zaib Un Nisa and others, 2026 SCMR 494, Civil Petition No. 74 of 2025.
  • Supreme Court Judgment Summaries 2026: The Voice of the Child Must Be Heard in Custody Cases (12 Oct 2026)
    The voice of the child must be heard in custody and guardianship cases: a binding obligation under the UN Convention on the Rights of the Child, not an aspiration, and the best interests of the child are paramount. Syed Mansoor Ali Shah and Aqeel Ahmed Abbasi, JJ, Dr Muhammad Asif vs Dr Sana Sattar and others, PLD 2026 SC 238 (C.R.P. No. 458 of 2024 in C.P.L.A. No. 2514 of 2024). Muhammad Hashim Khan Kakar, Salahuddin Panhwar aur Ishtiaq Ibrahim, JJ, Muhammad Kamran vs The State and Another, 2026 SCMR 205 (Criminal Appeal No. 91 of 2024).     Sach jaan kar di gayi ittila "jhooti" nahi, "sab-it na hui" hoti hai: Section 182 PPC sirf adalati amal se jhoot sabit hone par lagta hai. Irfan Saadat Khan aur Aqeel Ahmed Abbasi, JJ, Ali Adnan Sheikha vs I.G. Police Sindh, 2026 SCMR 77 (Cr.P.L.A. No. 147-K of 2025). Verified summary with LinkedIn breakdown link.

Yahya Afridi, CJ, Muhammad Shafi Siddiqui aur Miangul Hassan Aurangzeb, JJ, Mrs. Tasneem Abbas vs ADJ Lahore, 2026 SCMR 171 (C.A. No. 132-L of 2013). Verified summary with LinkedIn breakdown link.
