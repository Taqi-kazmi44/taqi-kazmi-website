Karachi: Two Men Sentenced to Life for Raping Minor Girl, Mother Jailed 3 Years

A mother who ignored her minor daughter's abuse has been jailed. A Karachi sessions court has sentenced two men to life imprisonment for sexually assaulting a minor girl, and jailed her mother for three years for allowing the abuse.

According to Dawn, Additional District and Sessions Judge (South) Abdul Zahoor Chandio found the victim's stepfather, Amjad Ali, and his accomplice, Zafar Iqbal, guilty. The minor girl's father had died, after which her mother remarried and the girl began living with her stepfather. The girl testified that her stepfather sexually assaulted her several times and that she told her mother, but her mother did not act. She further said her mother had taken Rs16,000 from Iqbal, who also sexually assaulted her. Her paternal uncle supported her in lodging the complaint, and a case was registered at the Defence police station.

Action taken so far: the court sentenced both men to life imprisonment and the mother, Suraiya, to three years in prison. Each man was directed to pay Rs500,000 to the victim in compensation, and the woman was fined Rs50,000. State prosecutor Irfana Qadri argued that the case was proved through the victim's direct testimony, corroborated by medical evidence and a DNA report. The DNA report matched the profile from the victim's frock with accused Zafar's blood sample. The accused denied the allegations and claimed they were falsely implicated over a dispute. The court rejected the defence.

What the law says: the case was registered under Sections 376(3), 109, 371-A, 371-B and 34 of the Pakistan Penal Code. Under Section 376(3), rape of a minor is punishable with death or imprisonment for life and fine. Sections 371-A and 371-B, selling and buying a person for prostitution, carry imprisonment of up to 25 years and fine. Imprisonment for life means 25 years under Section 57 of the Code, and the Supreme Court has held that a life prisoner must serve at least 15 years of actual imprisonment. Disclosing a rape victim's identity is itself an offence under Section 376-A, punishable with up to three years in prison and fine. That is why the victim is not named in this article.

What this means for you: a child who speaks up can get justice, and the law punishes not only the abuser but also those who enable the abuse. If a child tells you about abuse, report it to the nearest police station or call the police helpline 15, and get the child medical help and legal support immediately. A complaint can also be made by a relative, as the victim's uncle did here.
