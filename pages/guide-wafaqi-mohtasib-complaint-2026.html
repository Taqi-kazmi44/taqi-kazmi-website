How to File a Complaint with the Wafaqi Mohtasib: A Step-by-Step Guide

A federal department is ignoring your application, delaying your file or treating you unfairly? You can complain to the Federal Ombudsman (Wafaqi Mohtasib). It costs nothing, you do not need a lawyer, and cases are decided within 60 days.

The step-by-step procedure

  1. Complain to the department itself first. Write to the concerned federal agency (NADRA, the Passport Office, Pakistan Post, BISP, FBR and others) and keep the date and any reference number. The agency is bound to respond within 30 days; if it stays silent, your complaint moves to the Mohtasib's system on the 31st day.
  2. File your complaint with the Wafaqi Mohtasib. File online at the official website, through the Wafaqi Mohtasib mobile app, on helpline 1055 (1056 for complaints about children), by post or fax, by email, or in person at the head office in Islamabad (36 Constitution Avenue, G-5/2) or a regional office (Karachi, Lahore, Peshawar, Quetta and others).
  3. Attach the essentials. A copy of your CNIC, your first complaint to the department, any reply you received, and a clear statement of exactly what you want done.
  4. Track your case. You get a complaint number and SMS updates. The investigating officer calls for the department's report, may hold a hearing or inspect the site. Cases are decided within 60 days.
  5. Know the limits. The Mohtasib cannot touch Defence, Foreign Affairs, service matters of government employees, or matters already before a court. Provincial departments go to the provincial ombudsman, and banks go to the Banking Mohtasib.

The governing law

The procedure is governed by the Establishment of the Office of Wafaqi Mohtasib (Ombudsman) Order, 1983 (President's Order No. 1 of 1983). Article 9 empowers any person to complain to the Mohtasib against maladministration in a federal agency. Article 10 lays down how complaints are made, and Article 11 provides for investigation and decision within 60 days. The Federal Ombudsmen Institutional Reforms Act, 2013 strengthens the institutional framework, including the integrated complaint system under which a complaint first made to the agency itself must be answered within 30 days, failing which it transfers to the Mohtasib's CMIS on the 31st day. Filing is free of cost and no lawyer is required.

The key legal point

  1. What this means for you: maladministration in any federal department has a free, 60-day remedy. A short, dated complaint naming the exact office and stating plainly what you want is the fastest path to relief.
