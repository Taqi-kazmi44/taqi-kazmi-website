Karachi Q1 2026 Trend

Is crime falling in Karachi? The official data says yes. But look at the full picture.

Is crime falling in Karachi? The official data says yes. But look at the full picture.

The Sindh Police comparison of Q1 2026 against Q1 2025, for the Karachi Range: murder 127 against 137, motorcycle theft 2,290 against 3,119, bike snatching 722 against 1,071, car snatching 38 against 69. Most heads show a decline.

For law students: reading police statistics requires two questions. First, reported versus actual. Dawn reports that actual crime is far higher than reported, because victims do not report or the police refuse to register. Second, composition. Volume crime like theft fell, but kidnapping for ransom rose from 7 to 11, and rape and zina cases rose from 41 to 51. Critical reading of crime statistics is a basic skill of criminology.

For Karachi: the trend is improving, but the level is still high. Caution and the FIR are both necessary.
