Company Registration Pakistan through SECP

Register a company in Pakistan through SECP eZfile. Understand online company registration in Pakistan, SMC and private limited company documents before filing.

WhatsApp inquiryTaqi Kazmi website

What the process involves

  1. Name reservation on SECP eZfile
  2. Form 1 and incorporation particulars
  3. Promoters’ CNIC or passport copies
  4. Memorandum and articles generated or filed through the portal
  5. Current fee confirmed from the official SECP schedule before payment

Karachi coverage: DHA, Clifton, Saddar, Gulshan-e-Iqbal, North Nazimabad, PECHS aur pure Karachi se, aur overseas Pakistanis ke liye, SECP company registration mein madad faraham ki jati hai.

Kya SECP company search online ho sakta hai?
Ji haan, SECP ki website par company search ke zariye registered companies ki maloomat hasil ki ja sakti hain. Company search registration se pehle naam ki dastiyabi check karne mein bhi madadgar hai.
