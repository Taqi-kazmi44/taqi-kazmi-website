Jamshed Rashid v. The State: Supreme Court se pre-arrest bail ke do usool

FIR mein naam baad mein supplementary statement se dala gaya? Supreme Court ne June 2026 mein wazeh kar diya: ye kamzor saboot hai. Do usool: authenticated official record ko nazar andaz nahi kiya ja sakta, aur parity par bail milegi.

FIR mein aap ka naam baad mein, ek supplementary statement ke zariye dala gaya hai? Supreme Court ne June 2026 mein wazeh kar diya: ye kamzor saboot hai, aur adaalat is par aankhein band nahi kar sakti.

QANOON KYA KEHTA HAI: Jamshed Rashid v. The State mein Supreme Court ke do rukni bench (Justice Jamal Khan Mandokhail aur Justice Irfan Saadat Khan) ne Lahore High Court Multan Bench ka order khatam kar ke pre-arrest bail de di. Adaalat ne do usool tay kiye. Pehla: bail ke stage par authenticated official record, jaise travel documents, ko nazar andaz nahi kiya ja sakta. Agar official record kehta hai ke mulzim waqea ke waqt mulk mein tha hi nahi, to adaalat is saboot ko ignore nahi kar sakti. Doosra: asal FIR ke bajaye baad mein di gayi supplementary statement mein naam dalna kamzor evidence hai, jab tak is ki pusht par independent saboot na ho.

PARITY KA USOOL: Teen shareek mulziman ko pehle hi bail mil chuki thi, is liye adaalat ne barabari ke usool par darkhwast guzar ko bhi pre-arrest bail di. Yani jo relief ek ko mil chuka ho, wohi relief barabar ke case mein doosre ko bhi milna chahiye.

AAM GHALTI: Log samajhte hain ke FIR mein naam aa jane ka matlab giraftari ya saza pakki hai. Aisa nahi hai. Agar aap ko dushmani ki bina par jhooti FIR mein phansaya gaya hai to official record aur waqt par qanooni mashwara aap ka sab se bara hathyar hai.

Hawala: Jamshed Rashid v. The State, Supreme Court (detailed judgment, June 2026), APP report: https://www.app.com.pk/national/travel-record-cannot-be-ignored-supplementary-statement-is-a-weak-evidence-sc/
