How to Renew Your Pakistani Passport Online: DGIP Portal Steps

Is your Pakistani passport about to expire? You do not need to visit a passport office to renew it. The Directorate General of Immigration and Passports (DGIP) lets you renew your passport online from home. This guide walks you through the exact procedure on the official portal.

The step-by-step procedure

  1. Go to the official DGIP online portal only: onlinemrp.dgip.gov.pk. Do not use any other website that claims to renew passports, as fake sites charge extra.
  2. Register an account with your name, email address and phone number. If you are in Pakistan, you will receive a verification code by SMS and email. If you are abroad, the code comes by email only.
  3. Select the renewal category and enter your details exactly as they appear on your valid CNIC or NICOP and your existing passport. Any mismatch with your NADRA record can delay the application.
  4. Keep your documents ready before you start: your valid CNIC or NICOP, your existing passport, and a recent passport size photograph with a white background. Government employees also need a No Objection Certificate from their department.
  5. Complete fingerprint verification as the portal directs. Where offered, this is done through the NADRA Pak-ID mobile app. Otherwise, download the fingerprint form from the portal, capture your fingerprints, scan it, and upload it.
  6. Enter your delivery address carefully. This is the address where your printed passport will be delivered.
  7. Choose your passport options, such as the number of pages, validity period and processing speed. The fee is calculated by the portal according to the options you select, so check the amount and all your details before you pay by card.
  8. Review the application, sign the declaration and submit it. Save your PSID and application reference number so you can track the application on the portal.

Note

DGIP may still ask you to visit your chosen passport office for a short interview if it needs to verify something in person.

The governing source

The procedure is published by the Directorate General of Immigration and Passports (DGIP) on its Online MRP Passport Renewal Portal. Pakistani passports are issued under the Passports Act, 1974. No fee amount or processing time is stated here, because the fee depends on the pages, validity and speed you select and is calculated by the portal itself.

The key legal point

  1. What this means for you: if your details match your NADRA record, a renewal can be completed online without standing in a queue, as long as you prepare the CNIC, old passport, photograph and fingerprint verification first.
