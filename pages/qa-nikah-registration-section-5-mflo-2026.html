Is a Nikah Valid if It Is Not Registered?

Many people assume that a Nikah without a registered Nikah Nama is no Nikah at all. The law draws a different line: registration is compulsory, but it is not what makes a validly performed Nikah valid. Registration is the official record of the marriage, not the marriage itself.

The legal rule: registration is mandatory (Section 5(1))

Section 5(1) of the Muslim Family Laws Ordinance, 1961 provides: "Every marriage solemnized under Muslim Law shall be registered in accordance with the provisions of this Ordinance." For that purpose, the Union Council licenses Nikah Registrars. Under Section 5(3), every marriage not solemnized by the Nikah Registrar shall, for the purpose of registration, be reported to him by the person who has solemnized such marriage.

But non-registration does not by itself invalidate the Nikah

The Lahore High Court held in PLJ 2009 Lahore 574 that a marriage solemnized under Muslim law requires registration under Section 5(1), but the Nikah does not become invalid due to its non-registration. If a person does not report the marriage to the Nikah Registrar, he may be held liable under Section 5(4). The question of validity turns on whether the Nikah itself was validly performed, not on whether the paperwork was completed.

The penalty for failing to report (Section 5(4))

Section 5(4) provides that whoever contravenes Section 5(3) shall be punishable with simple imprisonment for a term which may extend to three months, or with fine which may extend to one thousand rupees, or with both. Note carefully what this penalises: it is the failure of the person who solemnized the marriage to report it to the Nikah Registrar, not a rule that the marriage itself disappears.

The key legal point

  1. What this means for you: an unregistered Nikah is not automatically void, but without a registered Nikah Nama it is much harder to prove the marriage and its terms when you need it for legal, family or official purposes, so registration should never be skipped. Keep your copy of the Nikah Nama safe, and get the marriage registered with the Union Council.
