Talaq Notice / Certificate

Muslim Family Laws Ordinance 1961 ki section 7 ke mutabiq talaq pronounce hone ke baad Chairman Union Council ko written notice dena lazmi hai. Statutory 90-day period se pehle talaq effective nahi hoti.

Qanooni procedure

  1. Talaq pronounce hone ke baad Chairman Union Council ko written notice dein.
  2. Notice ki copy wife ko supply karein aur delivery proof sambhal kar rakhein.
  3. Submitted copy aur Arbitration Council ki communication ka record rakhein.
  4. Statutory 90-day period complete hone se pehle final effectiveness assume na karein.
  5. Certificate issuance ka current Union Council process confirm karein.
  6. Case-specific advice aur current Union Council requirements alag ho sakti hain, verify zaroor karein.
