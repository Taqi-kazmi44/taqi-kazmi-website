How to Get a NADRA Family Registration Certificate (FRC) in Pakistan

Visa applications, immigration files, and many official processes often ask for one document: proof of your family relationship as recorded by NADRA. That document is the Family Registration Certificate (FRC). This guide walks you through how to get it, step by step.

The step-by-step procedure

  1. Choose the right type of FRC. FRC by birth lists parents and siblings for a person under 18. FRC by marriage lists a spouse and children. FRC by adoption lists a legal guardian and dependents.
  2. Check your NADRA record first. The FRC only shows what NADRA already has on record. Make sure every family member is correctly linked: CNIC, NICOP, or POC for adults, and the Child Registration Certificate (B-Form) for children. If a member is missing or wrongly linked, correct that record first.
  3. Keep your documents ready: your own CNIC, the CNICs or B-Forms of the family members to be listed, and a marriage certificate or birth certificate where applicable. If a deceased family member is to be shown correctly, NADRA needs the death certificate so the CNIC record can be cancelled and updated.
  4. Apply online through the official NADRA Pak Identity portal only: id.nadra.gov.pk. Log in, select the Family Registration Certificate option, enter the details exactly as they appear in NADRA's record, upload any documents or photograph the portal asks for, pay the fee shown by the portal, and submit. Save your tracking ID.
  5. Or apply in person at any NADRA Registration Centre. Complete biometric verification, and read the draft certificate carefully before it is printed. Check every name, CNIC number, and relationship at that stage.
  6. Collect and check the certificate. Online applicants receive the digital copy by email once the application is approved. In-person applicants receive the printed certificate at the centre after verification. Check it at once. If anything is wrong, first correct the underlying NADRA record, then apply for a fresh FRC.

Note

An FRC has no fixed expiry date, but it becomes outdated when your family record changes, such as a birth, marriage, death, or divorce. In that case, apply for a new one.

The governing source

The procedure is published by the National Database and Registration Authority (NADRA) for its Family Registration Certificate (FRC) service, on the official portal id.nadra.gov.pk. Fees and processing times can change, so check the fee shown by the portal or the centre before you pay.

The key legal point

  1. What this means for you: the FRC is not a new registration. It is a printout of your family record as NADRA holds it, so the real work is making sure that record is correct before you apply.
