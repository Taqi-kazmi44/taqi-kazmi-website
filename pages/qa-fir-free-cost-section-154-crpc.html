Is Registering an FIR Really Free in Pakistan?

One of the most common questions ordinary citizens ask: do I have to pay the police to register an FIR? The verified answer is no. Registering an FIR costs nothing, and asking for money in return is against the law.

The free FIR rule: Section 154 CrPC

Section 154(1) of the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898 says that every information relating to the commission of a cognizable offence, if given orally to an officer in charge of a police station, must be reduced to writing, read over to the informant, and signed. Then Section 154(2) says a copy of the information so recorded shall be given forthwith, free of cost, to the informant.

There is no fee for registering an FIR and no fee for your copy. The service is free because the FIR sets the criminal law in motion, and charging money for it would defeat that purpose.

If the police demand money or refuse to register

  1. Report to the SHO and the Superintendent of Police. Section 154(3) CrPC says a person aggrieved by a refusal to record the information may send the substance of it, in writing and by post, to the Superintendent of Police concerned, who must act on it if it discloses a cognizable offence.
  2. Call the police emergency helpline 15 and register your complaint about the refusal or the demand of money.
  3. Apply to the Sessions Judge under Sections 22-A and 22-B CrPC, who can order the police to register your FIR.
  4. Giving false information to the police is itself an offence, so always report true facts in your FIR.

The key legal point

  1. What this means for you: never pay anyone to get an FIR registered. The law gives you this service for free, and it gives you a remedy if the police refuse.
