Shc Constitutional Benches 2026

The system for constitutional cases at the Sindh High Court has changed.

The system for constitutional cases at the Sindh High Court has changed.

The Constitutional Benches of High Court of Sindh (Practice and Procedure) Act, 2025 (Sindh Act I of 2026), dated 26 January 2026, was made under Article 202A(6) and followed the 27th Amendment. The work of the SHC's Constitutional Benches will now be run by a committee: the Head of Constitutional Benches and two senior judges, who will decide the distribution of cases and the procedure.

The Sindh High Court sits principally at Karachi. Fundamental rights and writ petition cases will follow this new procedure, and every Karachi litigant and lawyer needs to know it.

Verify here (official Sindh Code, sindhlaws.gov.pk): https://www.sindhlaws.gov.pk/Setup/Publications_SindhCode/PUB-NEW-26-000068.pdf
