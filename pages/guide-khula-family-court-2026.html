How to File for Khula in Pakistan: A Step-by-Step Guide

Khula is the legal route by which a wife in Pakistan can end her marriage through the Family Court. The procedure has two stages: the court case, and then the Union Council certificate. Many people stop at the court decree and never complete the second stage, which leaves their legal status unfinished.

The step-by-step procedure

  1. File a suit for dissolution of marriage in the Family Court. State your grounds clearly, for example cruelty, desertion, failure of maintenance, or incompatibility. You may file through a lawyer.
  2. The court issues summons to the husband and makes a genuine effort at reconciliation between the parties.
  3. If reconciliation fails, the court can grant a decree of dissolution on the basis of khula. The husband's consent is not required. The Supreme Court has held that a wife's aversion is a valid ground where the marriage cannot continue within its limits (Khurshid Bibi v. Muhammad Amin, PLD 1967 SC 97).
  4. The decree is intimated to the Chairman of your Union Council. Under Section 7 of the Muslim Family Laws Ordinance, 1961, an Arbitration Council attempts reconciliation during a 90-day period.
  5. After 90 days, obtain the Certificate of Effectiveness of Divorce from the Union Council. This certificate is the final legal proof of dissolution, and you need it to update your NADRA record.

The governing law

The Family Court procedure is governed by the West Pakistan Family Courts Act, 1964. Statutory grounds for dissolution are listed in Section 2 of the Dissolution of Muslim Marriages Act, 1939. The Union Council stage is governed by Section 7 of the Muslim Family Laws Ordinance, 1961: notice to the Chairman, a 90-day reconciliation period before the Arbitration Council, and then the certificate of effectiveness. On the consent question, the Supreme Court held in Khurshid Bibi v. Muhammad Amin (PLD 1967 SC 97) that a wife can obtain khula even where the husband does not consent, if the court is satisfied the marriage cannot continue.

The key legal point

  1. What this means for you: a wife in Pakistan does not need her husband's permission to end an unworkable marriage, but the court decree alone is not the final step. The Union Council certificate after 90 days completes the process.
