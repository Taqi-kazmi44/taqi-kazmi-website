Family Law Part 10: Child Marriage (Sindh)

Sindh Pakistan ka pehla suba tha jis ne larkon aur larkiyon dono ke liye shaadi ki kam az kam umar 18 muqarrar ki, aur saza aksar logon ke andaze se kahin zyada sakht hai.

FAMILY LAW SERIES | Hissa 10 az 10

Sindh Pakistan ka pehla suba tha jis ne larkon aur larkiyon dono ke liye shaadi ki kam az kam umar 18 muqarrar ki, aur saza aksar logon ke andaze se kahin zyada sakht hai.

QANOON KYA KEHTA HAI: Sindh Child Marriages Restraint Act 2013 ke tehat child har wo shakhs hai, larka ya larki, jo 18 saal se kam umar ka ho. Jahan koi fareeq child ho wo child marriage hai.

SAZAEN: 18 se bara mard jo child marriage kare, use 2 se 3 saal sakht qaid aur jurmana ho sakta hai. Jo shakhs child marriage parhaye, karwaye ya us mein sahulat de, use bhi 2 se 3 saal sakht qaid aur jurmana hoga. Walidain ya sarparast jo child marriage ko farogh de, ijazat de, ya laparwahi se rokne mein nakam rahe, unhein 2 se 3 saal sakht qaid aur jurmana hoga, aur bache ke zimmedar shakhs ke khilaf qanooni mafrooza hoga.

PROCEDURE: Ye jurram qabil e giraftari, ghair zamanati aur ghair sulah-pazeer hai. Koi bhi shakhs judicial magistrate ko shikayat kar sakta hai, aur adaalat 90 din mein trial mukammal karegi.

FARQ: Purane federal Child Marriage Restraint Act 1929 mein larki ki umar sirf 16 thi. Sindh ne jan boojh kar sab ke liye 18 ka mayar buland kiya.

Hawala: Sindh Child Marriages Restraint Act 2013 (Dawn report): https://www.dawn.com/news/amp/1112097
