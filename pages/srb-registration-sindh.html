SRB Registration in Sindh: Sindh Sales Tax on Services

Sindh Sales Tax on Services Act 2011 ke tehat eSRB registration.

Sindh mein taxable services par SRB registration dhoondh rahe hain? Neeche official eSRB route aur required details di gayi hain.

WhatsApp inquiryCall 0311-2610683All services

Official procedure

  1. eSRB portal par New e-Registration select karke NTN enter karein.
  2. Registry, directors/shareholders, activities, branches aur bank account sections complete karein.
  3. Application verify aur submit karein; user ID/password email ya SMS par milta hai.
  4. NTN na ho to provisional certificate 30 din ya NTN issue hone tak mil sakta hai.

Kise chahiye

Sindh mein Second Schedule wali taxable services provide karne wale.

Required documents

  • Portal required documents dikhata hai
  • SST-01 mein entity, owners/directors, business places, utilities, bank aur declaration ki details li jati hain

Fee aur waqt: Fee as per official schedule. Full processing time to be confirmed.
