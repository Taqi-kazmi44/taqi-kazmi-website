Federal Ata Amendment 2025

Detention for 90 days without a charge? This law is now in force.

Detention for 90 days without a charge? This law is now in force.

The Anti-terrorism (Amendment) Act, 2025 brings back Section 11EEEE: the federal government or the armed forces may keep a terrorism suspect in preventive detention for up to 90 days, with reasons recorded in writing, while a Joint Interrogation Team (police, agencies, and armed forces) conducts the inquiry. The provision carries a 3-year sunset clause. Any detention beyond 3 months triggers Article 10 safeguards: informing the grounds, the right to counsel, and production before a magistrate within 24 hours.

Crimes like target killing, kidnapping for ransom, and extortion fall within its scope. Opposition parties questioned it on due process grounds, while the government says judicial oversight will prevent misuse.

Timeline: National Assembly on 13 August, Senate on 19 August, assent on 31 August 2025.
