Sindh Local Government (Amendment) Bill 2026: muddat khatam, kursi barqarar?

14 September 2026 ko Sindh Assembly ne manzoor kiya: council ki muddat khatam hone par outgoing mayor administrator ki haisiyat se kaam karta rahega. Jamaat-e-Islami ne Sindh High Court mein challenge kar diya hai.

Karachi ka mayor apni muddat khatam hone ke baad bhi kursi par reh sakta hai. Sindh Assembly ne isay qanoon bana diya.

14 September 2026 ko Sindh Assembly ne Sindh Local Government (Amendment) Bill 2026 manzoor kiya. Is ki sab se mutnazia shiq: council ki muddat khatam hone par outgoing mayor ya chairman administrator ki haisiyat se kaam karta rahega jab tak naya muntakhib sarbarah charge na le. Deputy mayor council ka convener banega.

Opposition ne aiwan mein ehtijaj kiya aur bill ki copies phaad di. Jamaat-e-Islami ab Sindh High Court gaya hai, uska moaqif hai ke ye amendment Constitution ke Article 140A ki khilaf warzi hai jo muntakhib numaindon ko ikhtiyarat ki muntaqili ka taqaza karta hai. Case pending hai.

Ye Karachi, Hyderabad aur Sindh ke har zila ko seedha mutasir karta hai. Adaalat ka faisla jo bhi ho, ye shiq tab tak nafiz rahegi jab tak khatam na ho ya mansookh na ki jaye.
