10 years in prison for rape: Section 376 PPC conviction in Karachi

A Karachi sessions court sentenced a man to 10 years imprisonment under Section 376 PPC with Rs 200,000 compensation to the survivor, reaffirming that delay in reporting a sexual offence is not automatically fatal.

Additional District and Sessions Judge (South) Abdul Zahoor Chandio sentenced convict Asad Anwar to 10 years imprisonment for raping a woman at a guest house in Clifton in 2025, and imposed a Rs 200,000 fine to be paid to the survivor as compensation.

The court noted it is settled law that delay in a sexual offence is not automatically fatal, because the victim or her family may take time to report the occurrence. The DNA examination report corroborated the survivor's statement, while CCTV footage and room booking details confirmed the accused's presence. The case was registered at the Boat Basin police station under Section 376 (rape) of the Pakistan Penal Code.
