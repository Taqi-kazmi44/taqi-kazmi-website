Does a Daughter Get a Share in Her Father's Property?

Yes. In the Islamic law of inheritance applied in Pakistan, a daughter has a fixed Quranic share in her father's property that no relative can legally take away. This page states the exact shares and the laws that protect them.

The rule, stated plainly

  1. Under Quran 4:11, a single daughter inherits one-half of the estate; two or more daughters together take two-thirds.
  2. In Pakistan these shares apply through the Muslim Personal Law (Shariat) Application Act 1937, which applies Quranic inheritance rules to all Muslims.
  3. The Supreme Court of Pakistan has repeatedly upheld the daughter's right (see Ghulam Ali v. Ghaznfar, PLD 1990 SC 1). Family pressure, or a document signed under pressure, cannot erase her fixed share.
  4. Depriving a daughter of her inheritance is a criminal offence under Section 498-A of the Pakistan Penal Code.
  5. What this means for you: no relative, brother, or family pressure can legally erase a daughter's share; she can claim it through the civil court.

The key legal points

  1. The share is fixed by Quran 4:11 and applies through the Muslim Personal Law (Shariat) Application Act 1937.
  2. Supreme Court authority (PLD 1990 SC 1) confirms the daughter's right cannot be defeated by family arrangement.
  3. Section 498-A PPC makes depriving a woman of her inheritance punishable.
  4. What this means for you: a daughter's property share is a legal right, not a favor that can be withdrawn.
