Supreme Court: gari ki tariff classification original design se hogi, modification se nahi

Gari ko stretch karke 10-seater bana liya, to kya tax bhi 10-seater wala lagega? Supreme Court ne jawab de diya: nahi. Collector of Customs, Peshawar vs Shahid Muhammad mein 3-rukni bench ne faisla diya.

Gari ko stretch karke 10-seater bana liya, to kya tax bhi 10-seater wala lagega? Supreme Court ne jawab de diya: nahi.

Collector of Customs, Peshawar vs Shahid Muhammad mein 3-rukni bench (CJP Yahya Afridi, Justice Muhammad Shafi Siddiqui, Justice Miangul Hassan Aurangzeb) ne faisla diya:

1. Tariff classification ORIGINAL DESIGN se hogi, baad ki private modification se nahi.
2. 5-seater ke tor par bani limousine PCT Heading 87.03 mein aayegi, 87.02 mein nahi.
3. Peshawar High Court ka 05-10-2023 ka order set aside kar diya gaya.
4. Faisla Justice Shafi Siddiqui ne tehreer kiya (7 safhaat).

Customs aur tax cases mein ye principle har importer ke kaam ka hai.
