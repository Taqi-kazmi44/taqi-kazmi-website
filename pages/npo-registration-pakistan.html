NPO Registration in Pakistan: Section 42 Non-Profit Company

Companies Act 2017 ki Section 42 ke tehat not-for-profit association ko company ke tor par license aur incorporate karna.

Agar aap Pakistan mein NGO ya NPO registration ki rehnumai search karke aaye hain to sahi jagah hain: NPO ke liye Section 42 company ka official route neeche diya gaya hai.

Official procedure

  1. Objects aur beneficiaries define karein; eligible promoters ready karein.
  2. SECP portal par company name reserve karein.
  3. App-3, supporting documents aur non-refundable licence fee submit karein.
  4. Licence ke 90 din ke andar company incorporate karein.
  5. Post-incorporation filings aur annual compliance maintain karein.

Kise chahiye

Kam se kam 3 eligible promoters jo welfare, education, health, arts, science ya similar non-profit objects par kaam karna chahte hain.

Required documents

  • App-3, IDs, CVs, qualifications/experience, notarized authority, draft MOA/AOA, affidavits aur case-specific approvals

Fee aur waqt: Section 42 licence aur incorporation fees revise ho sakti hain. Payment se pehle linked SECP guidebook aur latest official fee schedule par current amount confirm karein.

Aksar pooche jane wale sawalat

Section 42 company kya hoti hai?
Companies Act 2017 ki Section 42 ke tehat SECP se licence lekar banayi jane wali non-profit company hai. Ye apni amdani sirf apne maqasid par kharch karti hai aur members ko dividend nahi deti.

Section 42 licence ka process kya hai?
Pehle SECP ko licence ke liye application di jati hai jisme promoters ki maloomat, maqasid aur documents shamil hote hain. Licence milne ke baad SECP eZfile ke zariye company incorporate hoti hai.

NGO aur Section 42 NPO mein kya farq hai?
NGO Societies Registration Act 1860 ya trust ke tehat bhi ban sakti hai, jabke Section 42 route SECP-regulated company deta hai jo bare donors aur institutional grants ke liye aam tor par zyada mozoon samjhi jati hai.

Section 42 registration ke liye kaun se documents chahiye?
Promoters/directors ke CNIC, memorandum aur articles of association ka draft, maqasid ka bayan aur SECP ki darkaar deegar maloomat aur declarations darkar hote hain.
