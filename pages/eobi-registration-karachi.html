EOBI registration kis par lazim hai

Employees Old-Age Benefits Act, 1976 ke tehat har wo establishment jisme 5 ya zyada afrad mulazim hon, EOBI mein register hona lazmi hai. Registration na karane par jurmana aur qanooni karwai ho sakti hai. DHA, Clifton aur Saddar mein waqe companies, firms aur offices is zimmedari se mustasna nahi.

Registration ka procedure

  1. EOBI employer registration form (PR-02) pur karein
  2. Company ke registration documents (SECP certificate, partnership deed ya proprietorship proof)
  3. Malik ya authorized person ka CNIC
  4. Employees ki list aur unke CNIC copies
  5. Form EOBI ke mutalqa regional office ya online portal par jama karayein
  6. Registration ke baad mahana contributions waqt par ada karein aur record maintain rakhein

Contributions aur compliance

Registered employer ko har insured employee ke liye EOBI contribution ada karna hota hai. Contributions mein takheer par surcharge lag sakta hai. Baqaida record rakhna aur returns jama karana compliance ka hissa hai.

DHA, Clifton aur Saddar ki companies ke liye

DHA Karachi (Phase 1 se Phase 8), Clifton (Block 2, 5, 7, 8, Kehkashan) aur Saddar (Abdullah Haroon Road, Preedy Street, Raja Ghazanfar Ali Road) mein waqe companies, trading firms, consultants aur offices ke liye EOBI registration aur compliance ki sahulat Karachi se faraham ki jati hai. Aap ke office aaye baghair documents ki tayyari aur EOBI office follow-up mein madad ki jati hai.

Aksar pooche jane wale sawalat

Kya chhoti company ko bhi EOBI registration chahiye?
Ji haan, agar mulazmeen ki tadad 5 ya zyada hai to Act ke tehat registration lazmi hai, company ke size se qata nazar.

EOBI registration mein kitna waqt lagta hai?
Mukammal documents ke sath aam tor par chand hafton mein registration mukammal ho jati hai.

Kya purani non-compliance ka hal nikal sakta hai?
Ji, purani muddat ki contributions aur surcharge ka hisab laga kar compliance bahal ki ja sakti hai. Tafseel ke liye raabta karein.
