You never registered your rent agreement with the rent registrar. Should you panic?
The Supreme Court says: no, this defect can be cured.

In Tasneem Abbas vs ADJ Lahore, 2026 SCMR 171, the Court held that non-registration of a tenancy with the rent registrar is a curable defect. The person seeking eviction must be given a chance to fix it on payment of a fine; the case cannot be thrown out directly. The Court also held that a tenant's claim of ownership through a declaration suit does not stop eviction unless a decree is obtained. And in a memorable line, the Court warned that comparing handwriting oneself, without an expert, is dangerous and unreliable.

For law students, note Sections 5 and 9 of the 2007 Ordinance, and Article 84 of the Qanun-e-Shahadat. For tenants and landlords in Karachi and Lahore alike, the advice is practical: write the agreement and get it registered, that is your real protection.

Case: Tasneem Abbas vs ADJ Lahore, 2026 SCMR 171. Supreme Court of Pakistan, decided 10 September 2025. Bench: Yahya Afridi, CJ, Muhammad Shafi Siddiqui and Miangul Hassan Aurangzeb, JJ.
