LHC: Money Laundering Case Need Not Wait for Tax Case

Lahore High Court rules money laundering is a separate crime: FBR's DG I&I can register FIRs, investigate and prosecute under AMLA 2010 without waiting for income tax proceedings to conclude.

Your tax case is still pending. Can the FBR still start a money laundering case against you? The Lahore High Court says yes.

A two-member LHC bench comprising Justice Khalid Ishaq and Justice Hassan Nawaz Makhdoom issued the ruling in Writ Petition No. 2928 of 2026 and connected cases, dismissing all petitions that had challenged the powers of the FBR's Directorate General of Intelligence and Investigation, Inland Revenue.

Key holdings:
1. Money laundering is a separate crime in its own right. The FBR does not have to wait for an income tax case to conclude before starting a money laundering investigation.
2. The DG I&I has full legal authority to register FIRs, investigate and prosecute under the Anti-Money Laundering Act, 2010.
3. AMLA is a special law that takes precedence over general laws, so ongoing tax disputes cannot be used to stop or delay money laundering proceedings.
4. A person can be prosecuted for money laundering even without a prior conviction for the offence that generated the alleged proceeds.
5. The Supreme Court's Taj International decision was about sales tax assessment and recovery only, and cannot be used to block money laundering proceedings.
6. Suspicious transaction reports submitted by banks to the Financial Monitoring Unit operate within the legal framework, and writ jurisdiction cannot ordinarily halt a criminal investigation in advance.

The judgment stated: "Merely because the facts giving rise to a criminal prosecution may simultaneously entail civil or fiscal consequences does not denude the conduct of its criminal character."

Why it matters: tax proceedings and money laundering proceedings run on separate tracks. The FBR has clarified that if you declare your income and pay your taxes, this judgment changes nothing for you, but it strengthens action against those who hide illegal money or try to make it look legitimate. All such action must still follow due process and the rule of law.

Aam aadmi ke liye iska matlab: agar aap tax waqt par declare aur ada karte hain to is faislay se aap ko koi nuqsan nahi, lekin illegal raqam chhupane walon ke khilaf ab FBR tax case ka intezar kiye baghair karwai kar sakta hai.

Reference: Lahore High Court (Justices Khalid Ishaq and Hassan Nawaz Makhdoom), W.P. No. 2928 of 2026; reported by Business Recorder, 28 September 2026: https://www.brecorder.com/news/40441518
