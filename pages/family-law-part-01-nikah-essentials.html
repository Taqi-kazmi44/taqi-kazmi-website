Family Law Part 1: Nikah ke Sharaait

Bohat se log nahi jante ke Nikah ko qanoon ki nazar mein durust banane ke liye kya zaroori hai. Agar aap ke ghar mein kisi ka Nikah hone wala hai to ye batein samajhna lazmi hai.

FAMILY LAW SERIES | Hissa 1 az 10

Bohat se log nahi jante ke Nikah ko qanoon ki nazar mein durust banane ke liye kya zaroori hai. Agar aap ke ghar mein kisi ka Nikah hone wala hai to ye batein samajhna lazmi hai.

QANOON KYA KEHTA HAI: Pakistan mein Hanafi qanoon ke mutabiq Nikah ek civil contract hai. Durust Nikah ke liye chahiye: (1) ijab o qubool, yani offer aur acceptance ek hi mehfil mein, (2) contract ke waqt do gawah maujood hon, (3) biwi ke liye haq mehr tay ho, aur (4) dono fareeq ki razamandi aur qanuni haisiyat ho, aur rishta mamnoo darjon mein na ho.

AHM NUQTA: Muslim Family Laws Ordinance 1961 ke tehat Nikah ka registration alag lazmi zimmedari hai. Registration khud Nikah ko durust nahi banati, lekin is ke baghair aap adaalat mein apni shaadi sabit karne mein sakht mushkil ka samna karenge. Durust hona aur register hona do alag qanooni sawalat hain. Is series ke Part 2 mein Nikahnama tafseel se samjhaya gaya hai.

AAM GHALTI: Log taqreeb ko contract samajh lete hain. Bari taqreeb jahan proper ijab, qubool aur gawah na hon, qanoon ki nazar mein us sada Nikah se kamzor hai jahan ye shartein poori hon.

Hawala: Muslim Family Laws Ordinance 1961 (mukammal amended text): https://www.sja.gos.pk/assets/Updated_Laws/The_Muslim_Family_Laws_Ordinance_1961.pdf
