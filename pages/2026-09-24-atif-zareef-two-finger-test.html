Atif Zareef vs The State: two-finger test ghair-aaini qarar

Supreme Court ne 2021 mein two-finger test ko ghair-aaini aur ghair-qanuni qarar de diya. Court ne kaha ke is test ki koi scientific value nahi, aur ye rape survivor ki dignity (Article 14) ki khilaf warzi hai.

Ek rape survivor se kabhi poocha gaya tha ke wo virgin hai ya nahi.

Supreme Court ne kaha: ab kabhi nahi.

Atif Zareef vs The State (PLD 2021 SC 550, faisla 4 January 2021) mein Supreme Court of Pakistan ne two-finger test ko ghair-aaini aur ghair-qanuni qarar de diya. Court ne wazeh kaha ke is test ki koi scientific value nahi: hymen ki halat se ye sabit nahi hota ke rape hua ya nahi. Kisi survivor ko is test se guzarna uski dignity (Article 14) aur Constitution ke Article 4(2)(a) ke tehet tahaffuz ki khilaf warzi hai.

Faisla is se aage gaya. Court ne hukm diya ke adaalatein rape survivors ke liye 'habituated to sex', 'woman of easy virtue' ya 'non-virgin' jaise alfaaz istemal karna band karein, chahe ilzam sabit na bhi ho. Trial mein mulzim hai, mutasira nahi.
