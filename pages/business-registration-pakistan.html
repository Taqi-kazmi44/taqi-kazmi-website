Business Registration in Pakistan: Choose the Right Route

Pakistan mein business register karne ke teen aam routes hain: sole proprietorship (FBR), partnership firm (Registrar of Firms) aur private limited company (SECP). Sahi route ka intikhab liability, partners ki tadad aur business ke scale par munhasir hai.

Kaun sa route aap ke liye sahi hai? Teeno routes ka mukhtasar comparison aur har route ke dedicated page ke links neeche diye gaye hain.

Teen routes, aik nazar mein

Sole proprietorship: Aik fard ke liye sab se sada route. FBR NTN par business particulars add hote hain; business ki alag legal personality nahi hoti. Tafseel: Sole Proprietorship Registration Pakistan

Partnership firm: Do ya zyada partners ke liye. Partnership deed tayyar karke Registrar of Firms mein registration hoti hai. Tafseel: Partnership Registration Pakistan

Private limited company / SMC: SECP se incorporation. Company ki alag legal personality aur limited liability hoti hai; investment aur bare scale ke liye mozoon. Tafseel: SECP Company Registration Pakistan

Registration ke baad ki compliances

Business register honay ke baad NTN par annual income tax return, liability ki surat mein sales tax registration (FBR) ya SRB registration, aur employees hon to EOBI aur SESSI registration jaisi zimmedariyan aati hain. Brand name protect karna ho to trademark registration bhi karayi ja sakti hai.

Required documents

  • CNIC tamam routes mein lazmi hai
  • Sole proprietorship: bank certificate, premises proof
  • Partnership: partnership deed, partners aur gawahon ke CNIC
  • Company: promoters ke CNIC, memorandum aur articles (SECP eZfile)

Fee aur waqt: Fee as per official schedule. Processing time to be confirmed.

Aksar pooche jane wale sawalat

Pakistan mein business register karne ke kaun se tareeqe hain?
Teen aam routes hain: sole proprietorship (FBR NTN par), partnership firm (Registrar of Firms) aur private limited company (SECP). Har route ki liability, compliance aur taqaze mukhtalif hain.

Kaun sa route mere liye behtar hai?
Aik fard aur chhote scale ke liye sole proprietorship sada hai. Do ya zyada partners hon to partnership, aur limited liability, investment ya bare scale ki soorat mein private limited company zyada mozoon hai.

Business registration ke baad kya karna hota hai?
NTN par annual income tax return file karna hota hai. Liability ho to sales tax registration (FBR ya SRB), aur employees hon to EOBI aur SESSI registration jaisi compliances bhi shamil hain.

Kya overseas Pakistani Pakistan mein business register kar sakta hai?
Ji haan, overseas Pakistanis bhi FBR registration aur SECP company ke zariye Pakistan mein business register kar sakte hain. Kuch documents ki attestation ke additional steps ho sakte hain.
