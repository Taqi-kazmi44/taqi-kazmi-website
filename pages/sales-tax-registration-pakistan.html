Sales Tax Registration in Pakistan (FBR)

Sales Tax Act 1990 ke tehat goods par sales tax aur applicable Federal Excise Duty ki registration.

FBR sales tax registration ke liye sahi procedure yahan samjhein, phir WhatsApp par apna case discuss karein.

Official procedure

  1. Entity type aur liability verify karein.
  2. Tax House Facilitation Counter par relevant registration documents submit karein.
  3. Bank certificate, premises proof aur entity authorization complete rakhein.

Kise chahiye

Importers, wholesalers/distributors, qualifying manufacturers, Tier-1 retailers aur qanoon ke tehat liable persons.

Required documents

  • NTN registration wali entity checklist: bank certificate, premises evidence, recent utility bill aur entity documents

Aksar pooche jane wale sawalat

Sales tax registration (STRN) kis ko chahiye?
Importers, manufacturers, wholesalers/distributors, Tier-1 retailers aur Sales Tax Act 1990 ke tehat liable persons ko FBR se sales tax registration karani hoti hai.

Sales tax registration kahan aur kaise hoti hai?
FBR ke Iris portal ke zariye online application jama hoti hai. Pehle NTN registration mukammal hona chahiye, phir business particulars, bank certificate aur premises ke documents attach hote hain.

Sales tax registration ke liye kaun se documents chahiye?
NTN/CNIC, bank certificate, business premises ka saboot (ownership documents ya rent agreement), recent utility bill aur entity ke documents (partnership deed ya incorporation certificate) darkar hote hain.

Registration ke baad kya zimmedari hoti hai?
Registered person ko monthly sales tax return waqt par file karna aur record maintain rakhna hota hai. Takheer ya ghalti par penalty lag sakti hai, is liye registration ke sath return filing ka nizam bhi wazeh hona chahiye.
