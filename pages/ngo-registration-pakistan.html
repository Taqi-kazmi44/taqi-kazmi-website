NGO Registration Pakistan: Choose the Correct Legal Route

Register an NGO in Pakistan through a society, trust or Section 42 not-for-profit company. Compare the structure before preparing documents.

What the process involves

  1. Define objects, founders and operating area
  2. Compare society, trust and Section 42 company routes
  3. Prepare constitutional and identity documents for the selected authority
  4. Confirm current government fee and post-registration compliance before filing

Aksar pooche jane wale sawalat

Pakistan mein NGO banane ke kitne tareeqe hain?
Teen aam routes hain: Societies Registration Act (Sindh), SECP Section 42 company, ya Trust deed. Har route ke apne faide aur taqaze hain.

NGO registration mein kitna waqt lagta hai?
Route ke hisab se chand hafte se chand mahine lag sakte hain.

Kya NGO foreign funding le sakti hai?
Foreign funding ke liye alag approvals aur compliance darkar hoti hai.

Coverage: Pakistan bhar se NGO registration ke liye sahi legal route ke intekhab aur documents ki tayyari mein madad faraham ki jati hai.

Foundation registration: Welfare ya charitable maqasid ke liye foundation bhi SECP Section 42 company ya Trust ke zariye register hoti hai. Sahi route ke intekhab mein madad faraham ki jati hai.
