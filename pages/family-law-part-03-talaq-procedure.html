Family Law Part 3: Talaq ka Qanooni Tareeqa

Sirf talaq kehne se Pakistan mein shaadi khatam nahi hoti. Muslim Family Laws Ordinance 1961 ki Section 7 ek bilkul wazeh qanooni tareeqa muqarrar karti hai, aur is se hatna jurram hai.

FAMILY LAW SERIES | Hissa 3 az 10

Sirf talaq kehne se Pakistan mein shaadi khatam nahi hoti. Muslim Family Laws Ordinance 1961 ki Section 7 ek bilkul wazeh qanooni tareeqa muqarrar karti hai, aur is se hatna jurram hai.

QANOON KYA KEHTA HAI: Jo shaks kisi bhi soorat mein talaq de, use foran Chairman Union Council ko tehreeri notice dena hoga aur biwi ko us notice ki copy deni hogi. Talaq us waqt tak effective nahi hoti jab tak notice Chairman ko milne ke din se 90 din na guzar jayein, jab tak is muddat mein wapas na le li jaye.

PROCEDURE: Notice milne ke 30 din ke andar Chairman Arbitration Council banata hai jo dono mein sulah ki koshish karta hai. Agar biwi hamila hai to talaq 90 din ya hamal ke khatme ke baad effective hogi, jo baad mein ho. Effectiveness ke baad Chairman certificate of effectiveness of divorce jari karta hai, jis ki buniyad par NADRA computerized divorce certificate deta hai.

SAZA: Notice na bhejne par ek saal tak qaid, paanch hazaar rupay tak jurmana, ya dono ho sakte hain.

AAM GHALTI: Ek hi baar mein teen talaq de kar samajhna ke teenon talaqain poori ho gayin. Ordinance ke scheme ke tehat 90 din ka tareeqa effectiveness ko control karta hai, aur adaaltein ek mauqa par di gayi teen talaqon ko ek effective talaq qarar deti hain jo is muddat mein wapas li ja sakti hai.
