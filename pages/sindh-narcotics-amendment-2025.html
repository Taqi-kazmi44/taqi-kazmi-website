Sindh Narcotics Amendment 2025

Karachi is a major hub of the drug trade, and its governing law has just been updated.

Karachi is a major hub of the drug trade, and its governing law has just been updated.

The Sindh Assembly passed the Sindh Control of Narcotic Substances (Amendment) Act, 2025: passed on 28 July 2025, assent on 15 August, and notified on 18 August. It amends the 2024 provincial narcotics law, affecting the system of policing, punishments, and enforcement.

Karachi is a major transit and consumption point for narcotics. Every change in the law means the scope of police powers, arrests, and punishments can shift. For law students, this is an important case study in provincial criminal legislation.
