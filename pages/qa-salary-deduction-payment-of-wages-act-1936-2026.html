Can Your Employer Deduct Money From Your Salary in Pakistan?

One of the most common questions workers ask: can the employer cut money from the monthly salary, for a mistake, a fine, or any other reason? The answer is: not freely. The Payment of Wages Act, 1936 allows only the deductions it lists. This page states the rule exactly as the statute does.

What Section 7 of the Payment of Wages Act, 1936 says

Section 7(1) provides that the wages of an employed person shall be paid to him without deductions of any kind except those authorised by or under the Act. Section 7(2) then lists the only kinds of deductions that may be made: fines; deductions for absence from duty; deductions for damage to or loss of goods expressly entrusted to the employed person for custody, or for loss of money for which he is required to account, where such damage or loss is directly attributable to his neglect or default; deductions for house-accommodation supplied by the employer; deductions for amenities and services authorised by the Government; deductions for recovery of advances or adjustment of over-payments of wages; deductions of income-tax payable by the employed person; deductions required to be made by order of a Court or other competent authority; deductions for subscriptions to, and repayment of advances from, any provident fund; deductions for payments to co-operative societies or an insurance scheme; and deductions made with the written authorisation of the employed person for an approved savings scheme.

Section 8: fines have their own conditions

A fine is not free money for the employer. Under Section 8, no fine shall be imposed except for acts and omissions specified by notice with the previous approval of the Provincial Government or the prescribed authority, the notice must be exhibited on the premises, the employed person must be given an opportunity of showing cause against the fine, and the total fine in any one wage period shall not exceed half an anna in the rupee of the wages payable for that wage period.

What this means for you

Check your salary slip every month. If money has been cut, the employer must be able to point to one of the deductions listed in Section 7, and to the conditions for it, such as the Section 8 conditions for a fine. A deduction outside that list is not allowed. Section 15 of the Act provides for claims arising out of deductions from wages or delay in payment of wages, so a worker can raise a claim for an unauthorised deduction.

The key legal point

  1. No, your employer cannot deduct money from your salary freely: under Section 7 of the Payment of Wages Act, 1936, wages must be paid without deductions except those authorised by the Act.
  2. What this means for you: check your salary slip every month, and if money has been cut without a lawful reason, ask your employer in writing and keep the record.
