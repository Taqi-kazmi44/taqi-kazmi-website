KP Police Act 2026: Cabinet se approve, ab assembly ki bari

KP ki police ab CM ke board ko jawabdeh hogi. KP Cabinet ne 23 September 2026 ko revised KP Police Act 2026 approve kar diya. Ab ye draft assembly mein jayega. Note: ye cabinet approval hai, abhi qanoon nahi bana.

KP ki police ab CM ke board ko jawabdeh hogi.

KP Cabinet ne 23 September 2026 ko revised KP Police Act 2026 approve kar diya. Ab ye draft assembly mein jayega. Note: ye cabinet approval hai, abhi qanoon nahi bana.

AHAM TABDEELIYAN:

1. CM Police Policy Board ka chairman hoga, IG Police Operations Board ka sarbarah. Policy aur operations alag alag.

2. Subaai hakoomat federal police officers, bashamool IG, ki services unsatisfactory performance par wapas bhej sakegi.

3. IG ko har saal policing plan Board se approve karwana hoga. Har region mein Regional Investigation Officers tayunat honge, aur investigation aur dispute resolution ka nizam overhaul hoga.

Kya policy aur operations ko alag karne se thana culture theek hoga ya ye sirf kagzi islah rahegi? Comment mein raye dein.
