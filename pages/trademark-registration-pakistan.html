Trademark Registration in Pakistan (IPO)

Trade Marks Ordinance 2001 ke tehat Trade Marks Registry mein brand protection.

Apne brand ka trademark Pakistan mein register karwana hai? Official IPO route neeche samjhaya gaya hai.

WhatsApp inquiryCall 0311-2610683All services

Official procedure

  1. Optional TM-55 search se conflicting marks check karein.
  2. Har class ke liye TM-1 application file karein.
  3. Examination ke baad accepted application Journal mein publish hoti hai.
  4. Opposition na ho ya decide ho jaye to fee par registration aur certificate issue hota hai.

Kise chahiye

Individual, proprietor, AOP ya company jo apne mark ko specific goods/services classes mein protect karna chahe.

Required documents

  • Applicant details, goods/services, international class, mark representation, agent details if any, use/intention statement aur prescribed fee

Fee aur waqt: Trademark fees form aur class ke mutabiq revise ho sakti hain. Filing se pehle linked IPO-Pakistan fee schedule par current amount confirm karein.
