27Th Amendment 2025

Pakistan's Constitution has changed. And the change is bigger than most people realise.

Pakistan's Constitution has changed. And the change is bigger than most people realise.

The Constitution (Twenty-Seventh Amendment) Act, 2025 does five things at once. It creates a new Federal Constitutional Court. It amends Article 243: the Chief of Army Staff now becomes the Chief of Defence Forces, and the post of Chairman Joint Chiefs of Staff Committee stands abolished from 27 November 2025. It grants lifetime rank and privileges to the Field Marshal and equivalent ranks. It changes the system of judges' appointments and transfers. And it adds the Federal Constitutional Court to Article 6, the high treason provision.

The Senate passed it on 13 November 2025 with 64 votes, and it received assent the same day. This is the biggest constitutional change since the 18th Amendment. Two questions have changed at once: who interprets the Constitution, and who commands the military.
