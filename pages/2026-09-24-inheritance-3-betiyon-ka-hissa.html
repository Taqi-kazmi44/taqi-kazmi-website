Inheritance Law (3/7): Betiyon ka hissa, Quran aur Supreme Court ka faisla

Beti ko rukhsat ke naam par virasat se mehrum kar dena Pakistan ka sab se aam aur sab se ghalat rawaj hai. Surah An-Nisa (4:11) mein hissay muqarar hain, aur July 2026 mein Supreme Court ne is par mohar laga di hai.

INHERITANCE LAW SERIES | Hissa 3 az 7

Beti ko rukhsat ke naam par virasat se mehrum kar dena Pakistan ka sab se aam aur sab se ghalat rawaj hai. Quran ka hukm is par bilkul wazeh hai, aur July 2026 mein Supreme Court ne is par mohar laga di hai.

QANOON KYA KEHTA HAI: Surah An-Nisa (4:11) mein hissay muqarar hain. Do ya zyada betiyan hon to unhein kul jaidad ka do tihai milega. Akeli beti ho to us ka hissa aadha hai. Agar bete bhi hon to har beti ko bete ke hissay ka aadha milega. Ye hissay Allah ke muqarar karda hain, koi khandani faisla, dabao ya riwaj inhein khatam nahi kar sakta.

SUPREME COURT, JULY 2026: Do alag faisalon mein adaalat ne qaraar diya ke jhoote zabani tohfe (oral gifts), fareb par mabni transfer, khandani dabao ya riwaj aurat ko virasat se mehrum nahi kar sakte. Virasat ka haq maut ke waqt hi waris ko muntaqil ho jata hai. Jo shakhs dawa kare ke marhoom ne use zabani tohfa diya tha, use ye sabit karna hoga, aur adaalatein aurat ko mehrum karne wali har transaction ko sakhti se jancheingi. Koi jirga ya riwaj Islami qanoon e virasat par ghalib nahi aa sakta.

AAM GHALTI: Betiyon se khushi se dastakhat lekar hissa maaf karwana. Dabao mein liya gaya aisa dastakhat adaalat mein nahi tikta, aur dhoke se aurat ko virasat se mehrum karna PPC 498-A ke tehat jurm hai.

Hawala: Surah An-Nisa (4:11); SC faislay (July 2026): https://tribune.com.pk/story/2616057/sc-sets-rules-for-hiba-in-inheritance-cases ; https://www.nation.com.pk/01-Jul-2026/sc-upholds-women-s-inheritance-rights-overturns-71-year-old-rulings
