FBR ka social media income par naya tax procedure: YouTube views par deemed income

YouTube views par tax? Har 1,000 views par Rs195. FBR ne SROs 1640, 1641 aur 1642/2026 jari kar diye hain. Social media se kamane walon ke liye naya tax procedure aa gaya hai, Section 99C aur Section 237, Income Tax Ordinance, 2001 ke tehet.

QANOON KYA KEHTA HAI: Aapki income wo rakam hogi jo ZYADA ho, aapki actual earning ya deemed formula (har 1,000 YouTube views par Rs195). Expenses revenue ke 30% se zyada claim nahi ho sakenge. Advance tax har quarter mein Section 147 ke tehet dena hoga. Kam declare kiya to Commissioner return rectify karke farq recover kar sakta hai.

Matlab ab tax sirf bank mein aane wali rakam par nahi, views par bhi lagega.

Kya views par deemed income lagana munsifana hai? Comment mein raye dein.
