Sole Proprietorship Registration in Pakistan (FBR)

Sole proprietorship Pakistan mein business shuru karne ka sab se sada route hai. Business ki alag legal personality nahi hoti; malik apne CNIC aur NTN par FBR mein apna business register karta hai.

Apne naam par shop, trading, online selling ya services ka business shuru karna hai? Procedure aur documents neeche diye gaye hain.

Official procedure

  1. CNIC par registered SIM, personal email aur bank account certificate tayyar karein.
  2. FBR Iris portal par e-enrollment karein; individual ke liye CNIC number hi NTN hota hai.
  3. Iris profile mein business particulars add karein: business name, address aur activity (Form 181).
  4. Business bank account khulwayein aur zarurat par local trade license hasil karein.
  5. Sales tax liability ho to FBR sales tax registration alag se karayein.

Kise chahiye

Aik fard jo shop, trading, online selling, freelancing ya services ka business apne naam par chalana chahta hai.

Required documents

  • CNIC
  • Bank account certificate
  • Registered SIM aur email
  • Business premises proof (ownership ya rent agreement) aur recent utility bill jahan applicable

Aksar pooche jane wale sawalat

Sole proprietorship kya hoti hai?
Ye business ki sab se sada legal soorat hai jisme business aur malik aik hi shakhs hote hain. Business ki alag legal personality nahi hoti aur tamam zimmedariyan malik ki apni hoti hain.

Sole proprietorship ki registration kahan hoti hai?
FBR ke Iris portal par NTN registration ke sath business particulars add karke registration mukammal hoti hai. Sole proprietorship SECP mein register nahi hoti.

Sole proprietorship ke liye kaun se documents chahiye?
CNIC, bank account certificate, registered SIM, email, aur business premises ka saboot (ownership documents ya rent agreement) aur recent utility bill jahan applicable ho.

Kya sole proprietor ko income tax return file karna hota hai?
Ji haan, business income par annual income tax return file karna hota hai. Filer status se bank, gari aur property transactions mein bhi sahulat rehti hai.

Sole proprietorship aur company mein kya farq hai?
Private limited company ki alag legal personality hoti hai aur shareholders ki liability limited hoti hai, jabke sole proprietorship mein malik ki personal zimmedari unlimited hoti hai.
