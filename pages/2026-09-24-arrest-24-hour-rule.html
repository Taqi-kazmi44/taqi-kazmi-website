Giraftari ke 24 ghante: police aapko baghair magistrate ke nahi rakh sakti

Article 10(2) aur CrPC Section 61 wazeh hain: har giraftar shakhs ko giraftari ke 24 ghante ke andar magistrate ke samne pesh karna lazmi hai. Is muddat ke baad baghair magistrate ke hukum hirasat ghair-qanuni hai.

Pakistan mein police giraftar karke aapko 24 ghante se zyada nahi rakh sakti. Zyada tar log ye haq jante hi nahi.

Constitution ka Article 10(2) aur Criminal Procedure Code 1898 ki Section 61 wazeh hain: har giraftar shakhs ko giraftari ke 24 ghante ke andar magistrate ke samne pesh karna lazmi hai. Is muddat ke baad magistrate ke hukum ke baghair hirasat ghair-qanuni hai.

Article 10(1) do aur tahaffuz deta hai: giraftari ki wajah jald az jald batana lazmi hai, aur aapko apni pasand ka wakeel rakhne aur mashwara karne ka haq hasil hai.

Agar investigation ke liye aur waqt chahiye to police ko Section 167 CrPC ke tehet remand lena hoga, aur magistrate ko wajah tehreer mein darj karni hogi. Remand istisna hai, usool nahi.
