Inheritance Law (6/7): Waris ko mehrum karna mumkin nahi, aur jurm bhi

Main apni beti ko jaidad se aaq kar dunga. Qanoon is ka jawab deta hai: aaq karne se virasat ka haq khatam nahi hota. Aur dhoke se aurat ko virasat se mehrum karna PPC 498-A ke tehat jurm hai.

INHERITANCE LAW SERIES | Hissa 6 az 7

Main apni beti ko jaidad se aaq kar dunga. Ye jumla Pakistan ke hazaaron gharon mein bola jata hai. Qanoon is ka jawab deta hai: aaq karne se virasat ka haq khatam nahi hota.

QANOON KYA KEHTA HAI: Waris ko wasiyat ke zariye mehrum nahi kiya ja sakta. Kisi waris ke haq mein ki gayi wasiyat doosre warison ki ijazat ke baghair invalid hai (Ghulam Muhammad v. Ghulam Hussain, 1932 PC 81). Zindagi mein diya gaya asli tohfa (hiba) alag cheez hai aur durust hai, lekin July 2026 mein Supreme Court ne wazeh kiya ke aurat ko mehrum karne ke liye banaye gaye jhoote zabani tohfe aur farebi transfer adaalatein khatam kar dengi. Aise har transaction ko sakhti se jancha jayega.

JURM BHI HAI: Anti-Women Practices law ke tehat Pakistan Penal Code ki dafa 498-A mein aurat ko dhoke se us ki virasat se mehrum karna jurm hai, jis par 10 saal tak qaid aur 10 lakh rupe tak jurmane ki saza ho sakti hai. Yani ye sirf deewani jhagra nahi, faujdari case bhi ban sakta hai.

AAM GHALTI: Beti ya biwi se zabardasti maafi nama likhwa lena. Dabao mein liya gaya dastakhat adaalat mein nahi tikta, aur aisa karne wala khud mujrim ban sakta hai.

Hawala: Ghulam Muhammad v. Ghulam Hussain, 1932 PC 81; SC hiba faisla (July 2026): https://tribune.com.pk/story/2616057/sc-sets-rules-for-hiba-in-inheritance-cases ; PPC 498-A
