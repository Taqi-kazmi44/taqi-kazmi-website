FIR Kaun De Sakta Hai? Har Shakhs Ka Haq

Aam ghalat fehmi hai ke FIR sirf mutasira shaks de sakta hai. Qanoon is se kahin wasee hai: CrPC ki Section 154 kehti hai ke cognizable offence ki 'every information' SHO likhne ka paband hai.

Qanoon kya kehta hai

  1. Section 154 CrPC ke tehet FIR koi bhi de sakta hai: mutasira, gawah, rishtedar ya koi ajnabi.
  2. Shart sirf ek hai: ittila se cognizable jurm zahir ho.
  3. SHO FIR darj karne se pehle ye tehqeeq nahi kar sakta ke baat sach hai ya jhoot (PLD 2005 SC 297).
  4. Yaad rakhein: FIR ka darj hona ilzam hai, jurm ka saboot nahi.

Police inkar kare to kya karein

  1. Agar police FIR darj karne se inkar kar de to CrPC 22-A(6)(i) ke tehet Sessions Judge (ex-officio Justice of Peace) se ruju karein.
  2. Sessions Judge police ko FIR darj karne ka hukum de sakta hai.

Khawateen ke liye khaas tahaffuz

  1. 2016 ki tarmeem ke baad PPC 336B, 354, 354A, 376 aur 509 ke cases mein aurat ki ittila female officer ya ghar ki khatoon ki maujoodgi mein record hogi.
