Time-Scale Promotion: Haq Wapas Nahi Cheena Ja Sakta

Supreme Court ne sarkari mulazimeen ke haq mein ek bara faisla de diya. Time-scale promotion ki shartein poori karne walon se nayi policy peeche se laga kar haq nahi cheena ja sakta.

Supreme Court ne sarkari mulazimeen ke haq mein ek bara faisla de diya.

Agar aap ne ya aap ke kisi azeez ne time-scale promotion ki tamaam shartein poori kar li hain, to koi nayi policy peeche se lag kar ye haq nahi cheen sakti.

Key points:
- Supreme Court ke 3-judge bench ne 23 se zyada civil petitions ka faisla sunaya.
- Bench: Justice Muhammad Ali Mazhar (author), Justice Irfan Saadat Khan aur Justice Shahid Bilal Hassan.
- Justice Mazhar ne 8-page judgment mein kaha: "once a beneficial OM was issued by the competent authority, it was then their responsibility to implement such an OM in letter and spirit, without providing any cause of complaint to the employees."
- Purani Office Memorandums (Dec 2011, 2015, March 2021) ke zamanay mein jo rights mil chuke thay, wo May 2025 ki nayi policy se khatam nahi ho sakte. Qanun peeche nahi lagta.
- PM Office, Establishment Division, Finance, FPSC aur Maritime Affairs samet tamam departments ko 3 mah mein har employee ka case alag alag dekh kar written speaking order dena hoga.

Aam aadmi ke liye iska matlab: mulazimat mein diya gaya wada wada hota hai. Departments ko apni hi banai policy par poori tarah amal karna parega.
