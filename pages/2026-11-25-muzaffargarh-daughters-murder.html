Muzaffargarh: Father Allegedly Kills Two Minor Daughters, Injures Wife

Two little girls, aged three and five, were killed by their own father. Their only crime was trying to save their mother.

The incident took place in Kachhi Lal area of Seetpur, Alipur tehsil, in Muzaffargarh district. According to police, the man wanted to contract a second marriage because he had no son from his first wife. His first wife objected to the second marriage, and harsh words were exchanged between them.

The man then allegedly attacked his wife with a hammer, and she suffered critical injuries. When his two daughters, aged three and five years, tried to stop their father, he allegedly pulled out a knife and slit their throats. He then attempted to slit his own throat and was injured.

Action taken so far: police and rescue teams shifted the injured woman to hospital for treatment, and registered a murder case against the suspect on behalf of the state at Seetpur police station. Muzaffargarh District Police Officer Syed Ghazanfar Shah took notice of the incident and directed officials to arrest the suspect. The suspect has been arrested and admitted to hospital for treatment.

What the law says: murder is punishable under Section 302 of the Pakistan Penal Code with death or imprisonment for life. An attack with the intention to kill, such as the attack on the wife, is separately punishable as an attempt to commit murder under Section 324 of the Pakistan Penal Code, which carries imprisonment of up to ten years and a fine. The sections of the FIR have not been reported in the press, so no FIR sections are stated here. No family dispute, and no desire for a son, can justify taking a life. A daughter is not a lesser child. The allegations against the arrested man are yet to be proved in court, and he remains innocent until proven guilty.

What this means for you: if you witness violence against a woman or a child, report it at once. Call police helpline 15, or the Child Protection and Welfare Bureau helpline 1121 for a child in danger. You can also complain to the District Police Officer of your district, and if the police refuse to register an FIR, an application under Section 22-A of the Code of Criminal Procedure can be filed before a Justice of the Peace for registration of the case.
