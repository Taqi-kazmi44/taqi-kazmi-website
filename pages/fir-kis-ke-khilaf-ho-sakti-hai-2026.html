FIR Kis Ke Khilaf Ho Sakti Hai?

Naam maloom na ho to FIR nahi ho sakti? Ho sakti hai. Aur sirf aam shaks hi nahi, company aur sarkari officer ke khilaf bhi FIR ka darwaza khula hai.

Naam maloom na ho to

  1. Section 154 CrPC sirf ye mangti hai ke ittila se cognizable jurm zahir ho.
  2. Mulzim ka naam maloom hona shart nahi, isi liye 'namaloom afraad' ke khilaf FIR roz ka mamul hai.

Company ya idare ke khilaf

  1. PPC ki Section 11 kehti hai 'person' mein company ya association bhi shamil hai, is liye company ke khilaf FIR ho sakti hai.
  2. Officers ki zimmedari ka faisla jurm ke ajza aur har officer ke mubinah kirdar par munhasir hoga; ye khudkaar nahi hoti.

Sarkari mulazim ya police officer ke khilaf

  1. CrPC ki Section 197 sirf ye kehti hai ke adalat sanction ke baghair case ka cognizance nahi le gi.
  2. FIR darj karne ya tafteesh par koi pabandi nahi (PLD 2022 Lahore 224).
  3. Lekin yaad rahe: FIR mein naam aana jurm ka saboot nahi.
