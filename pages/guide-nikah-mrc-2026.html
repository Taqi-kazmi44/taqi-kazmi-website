How to Register Your Nikah and Get a NADRA Marriage Registration Certificate (MRC) in Pakistan

Your Nikah is solemnised, but is it registered? Without registration, you cannot get a passport or a visa, and you cannot prove your marriage to any government office. Registration is not optional in Pakistan. Here is how to do it, step by step.

Step by step

  1. Get the Nikah solemnised by a licensed Nikah Registrar. The registrar prepares the Urdu Nikah Nama on the prescribed form and signs it, along with the bride, the groom and two witnesses. Check every entry, especially names, CNIC numbers and the Mehr amount, before signing.
  2. Make sure the Nikah Nama reaches the Union Council. The Nikah Registrar sends a copy to the Union Council of the area where the Nikah was solemnised. If the Nikah was not solemnised by a registrar, the person who solemnised it must report it to the registrar for registration. Follow up yourself and confirm the record exists.
  3. Apply for the computerised Marriage Registration Certificate (MRC). Go to the same Union Council, or the Tehsil Municipal Administration, Cantonment Board, or Arbitration Council in Islamabad, that registered the Nikah. Take the original Urdu Nikah Nama, CNIC copies of both spouses and the CNIC copy of the Nikah Registrar.
  4. Pay the prescribed fee at the counter and keep the receipt. The fee is fixed by the local authority and varies, so confirm the current amount at your Union Council before you go.
  5. The office enters your record into NADRA's Civil Registration Management System, verifies it, and prints the computerised, bilingual MRC with a QR code. Collect it and check the names, dates and CNIC numbers at the counter.
  6. If your Nikah was never registered, it can still be registered late. Go to the same Union Council with the original Nikah Nama and CNICs and apply for late registration, and the office will verify the record and complete it.
  7. Keep both documents safe. The Urdu Nikah Nama is your original marriage contract, and the computerised MRC is the proof embassies, NADRA and passport offices ask for. For use abroad, the MRC usually needs attestation from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

What happens if a marriage is not registered?

Failing to register a marriage is an offence. A person who does not report the marriage for registration can face simple imprisonment up to three months, or a fine up to Rs 1,000, or both.

Reference

What this means for you: a registered Nikah protects both spouses, because only a registered marriage gives you a computerised certificate that the state, NADRA and embassies accept.
