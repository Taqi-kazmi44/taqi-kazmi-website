FAMILY LAW SERIES | Hissa 9 az 10

Baghair Arbitration Council ki ijazat ke doosri shaadi Pakistan ke family law ka sab se ghalat samjha jane wala hissa hai. Nateeje bohat sakht hain, aur January 2026 mein Supreme Court ne is par ek ahm faisla bhi diya hai.

QANOON KYA KEHTA HAI: Muslim Family Laws Ordinance 1961 ki Section 6 kehti hai ke koi mard maujuda shaadi ke dauran Arbitration Council ki pehle se tehreeri ijazat ke baghair doosri shaadi nahi kar sakta. Baghair aisi ijazat ki gayi shaadi Ordinance ke tehat register bhi nahi hogi.

PROCEDURE: Shohar Chairman ko darkhwast deta hai jis mein wajuhat aur ye bayan karta hai ke pehli biwi ki razamandi hasil hui hai ya nahi. Chairman dono taraf se numainday mangwata hai, aur Arbitration Council ijazat sirf tab deta hai jab mutmain ho ke majooza shaadi zaroori aur munsifana hai.

NATEEJE: Jo mard baghair ijazat doosri shaadi kare use maujuda biwi ya biwiyon ka poora mehr, prompt ya deferred, foran ada karna hoga, jo land revenue ke baqayajat ki tarah wasool hoga. Conviction par maujuda amended text ke tehat ek saal tak qaid aur jurmana ho sakta hai. January 2026 mein Supreme Court ne isi lazmi tareeqe par amal na hone ki wajah se ek shaadi khatam kar di.

AAM GHALTI: Samajhna ke ghair registered doosri Nikah "shumar nahi hoti". Shaadi ek haqiqat ke taur par maujood ho sakti hai, lekin mehr aur faujdari nateeje registration se qata nazar lagte hain.

Hawala: Muslim Family Laws Ordinance 1961, Section 6 (amended text): https://www.sja.gos.pk/assets/Updated_Laws/The_Muslim_Family_Laws_Ordinance_1961.pdf
