LHC Bans All Filming Inside Court Premises, Warns of Phone Confiscation

The Lahore High Court has prohibited all video recording and photography across its premises, including courtrooms, corridors, waiting areas and offices, under a circular approved by Chief Justice Aalia Neelum. A mobile phone used for unauthorised recording may be confiscated, and strict action will be taken against violators. The ban applies to lawyers, police officials, journalists, litigants and visitors, with only the journalists' designated area exempted.

The Lahore High Court has banned all video recording and photography inside its premises. In a circular approved by Chief Justice Aalia Neelum and notified by the Lahore High Court Registrar, filming and photography are now prohibited across the entire court premises, including courtrooms, corridors, waiting areas and offices.

The ban applies to everyone: lawyers, police officials, journalists, litigants and visitors. The only exception is the area specifically designated for journalists.

A mobile phone used for unauthorised recording may be confiscated. Strict action will be taken against violators in accordance with law, and Registrar Abdul Rahim has ordered strict implementation of the circular.

For ordinary visitors, this means: keep your phone in your pocket when visiting the High Court, because recording a video or taking a photo inside can lead to your phone being seized.
