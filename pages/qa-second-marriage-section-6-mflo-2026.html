Can a Second Marriage Be Contracted Without Permission?

Many people ask whether a man can marry a second time on the first wife's informal consent alone. The law draws a clear line: the permission that matters is the previous permission in writing of the Arbitration Council, obtained before the second marriage.

The legal rule: Section 6, Muslim Family Laws Ordinance, 1961

Section 6(1) provides that no man, during the subsistence of an existing marriage, shall, except with the previous permission in writing of the Arbitration Council, contract another marriage. Under Section 6(2), the application for permission is submitted to the Chairman in the prescribed manner, together with the prescribed fee, and states the reasons for the proposed marriage and whether the consent of the existing wife or wives has been obtained. Under Section 6(3), the Arbitration Council so constituted may, if satisfied that the proposed marriage is necessary and just, grant the permission applied for, subject to such conditions as may be deemed fit.

Consequences of marrying without permission: Section 6(5)

Any man who contracts another marriage without the permission of the Arbitration Council shall (a) pay immediately the entire amount of the dower, whether prompt or deferred, due to the existing wife or wives, which amount, if not so paid, shall be recoverable as arrears of land revenue; and (b) on conviction upon complaint be punishable with simple imprisonment which may extend to one year, or with fine, or with both. The original fine in the Ordinance was Rs. 5,000; in Punjab it was increased to Rs. 500,000 by the Punjab Muslim Family Laws (Amendment) Act, 2015. The absence of permission does not by itself make the second Nikah void, but it leaves the husband exposed to the criminal and dower consequences above.

What this means for you

  1. A second marriage is not a private decision alone. The written permission of the Arbitration Council must come first, otherwise the full dower becomes payable at once and a criminal complaint can follow.
