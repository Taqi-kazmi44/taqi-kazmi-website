Foundation Registration in Pakistan

A foundation is not one universal legal form in Pakistan. Compare society, trust and Section 42 charitable organization registration routes.

What the process involves

  1. Define the charitable or public-benefit objects
  2. Choose society, trust or Section 42 company as the legal vehicle
  3. Prepare governance, office and founder or trustee records
  4. Confirm tax and annual compliance after registration
