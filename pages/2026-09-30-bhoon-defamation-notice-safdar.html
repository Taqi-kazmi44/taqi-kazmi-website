Ahsan Bhoon Serves Rs5 Billion Defamation Notice on Capt Safdar Under Punjab Defamation Act 2024

Ahsan Bhoon, former SCBA president and Judicial Commission member, has served a Rs5 billion defamation notice on Capt (retd) Safdar over public claims about his LLB degree, demanding retraction and a public apology within seven days or proceedings under the Punjab Defamation Act, 2024.

Ahsan Bhoon, former president of the Supreme Court Bar Association and a sitting member of the Judicial Commission of Pakistan, has served a legal notice on Capt (retd) Safdar seeking Rs5 billion in damages.

The dispute goes back to September 23, when Safdar, speaking outside Parliament House, publicly questioned the authenticity of Bhoon's LLB degree. He also alleged that SCBA presidential candidate Amjad Shah holds a fake degree.

The notice demands a retraction and a public apology within seven days, and asks that the claims stop. Failing that, Bhoon will initiate defamation proceedings under the Punjab Defamation Act, 2024, and possible criminal proceedings.

Why it matters: defamation law protects reputation. An unverified public allegation about someone's professional credentials is not free speech. Under the 2024 Act, damages can run into billions, and a public apology is the minimum the law expects before trial.
