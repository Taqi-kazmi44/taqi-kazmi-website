How to Get a Police Character Certificate in Pakistan: A Step-by-Step Guide

A visa, a job abroad, or an immigration file often asks for one document first: a Police Character Certificate. It confirms your police record status. In Punjab, you do not need to travel to the district of your old address to get it. This guide walks you through the exact procedure published by the Punjab Police Khidmat Markaz.

The step-by-step procedure

  1. Go to any Police Khidmat Markaz in Punjab. You can apply from any centre in the province, even if the certificate relates to a different district where you lived before.
  2. Take your documents. You need your original CNIC or B-Form with a photocopy, your original passport with a copy, and an affidavit. If the certificate is for a blood relative, also take a photograph of that relative.
  3. Pay the processing fee of Rs. 500 at a counter of the Khidmat Markaz.
  4. Submit the application and note the delivery method. The certificate is delivered by courier.
  5. Wait for the turnaround time of 3 working days after the date of application.
  6. If you are abroad, a blood relative (father, mother, brother or sister) can submit the form for you with your authority letter. The authority letter must carry the stamp of the relevant Embassy, or be attested by two respectable persons who know you, and it must mention the duration of your stay.

The governing source

The procedure, documents, fee and turnaround time are published by the Punjab Police Khidmat Markaz on its official Police Character Certificate service page: original CNIC or B-Form and photocopy, original and copy of passport, affidavit, authority letter where the applicant is abroad, Rs. 500 processing fee, 3 working days turnaround, and courier delivery.

The key legal point

  1. What this means for you: gather the CNIC, passport and affidavit before you go, and a single visit to any Khidmat Markaz in Punjab is enough to start the process.
