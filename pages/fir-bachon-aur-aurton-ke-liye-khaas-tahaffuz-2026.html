Bachon Aur Aurton Ke Liye Qanoon Ka Khaas Tahaffuz

Qanoon bachon aur aurton ko khaas tahaffuz deta hai. Ye maloomat tahaffuz ke liye hai; giraftari ya zamanat ka faisla adalat karti hai.

Bachon ke liye tahaffuz

  1. PPC Section 82: 10 saal se kam umar ke bache ka koi fel jurm nahi.
  2. PPC Section 83: 10 se 14 saal tak ka bacha, agar samajh na rakhta ho to us ka fel bhi jurm nahi.

Juvenile Justice System Act 2018

  1. 18 saal se kam umar 'child' hai aur us ka case Juvenile Court sune gi.
  2. Jis ne jurm ke waqt 18 saal poore na kiye hon, usay maut ki saza nahi ho sakti.
  3. Hathkari ya beriyan aam tor par mamnu hain; sirf 16 saal se bara juvenile jo sangin jurm mein mulawwis ho aur faraar ka khatra ho, us par adalat ki ijazat se pabandi lag sakti hai.

Aurton ke liye tahaffuz

  1. CrPC Section 52: aurat ki talashi sirf aurat le gi, poori tehzeeb ke sath.
  2. 2016 ki tarmeem ke baad makhsoos cases mein aurat ka bayan female officer ya us ki pasand ke shakhs ki maujoodgi mein record hoga.

Ek ghalat fehmi ki wazahat

  1. Pakistan ke CrPC ki Section 160 mein aurton ya buzurgon ke liye koi riayat wali shart nahi; ye India ke qanoon ki baat hai.
