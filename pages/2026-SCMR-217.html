2026 SCMR 217

He was in the truck, but the drugs were hidden in secret compartments.
Is mere presence alone a crime? The Supreme Court said no.

In Khadim Hussain vs The State, 2026 SCMR 217, 15 kilograms of narcotics, heroin, ice, opium, and chars, were hidden in the truck's secret compartments. The Court held that merely being in the truck is not enough unless conspiracy or abetment is proved. Article 122 of the Qanun-e-Shahadat, on exclusive knowledge, does not apply where knowledge itself is not proved. Criminal liability is individual, not collective. It was a case of further inquiry, and bail was granted.

For law students, the pillars here are actus reus plus mens rea, the scope of Article 122 QSO, and Section 497(2) CrPC. For citizens, the principle protects you: travelling with someone does not make you a criminal; knowledge and intention must be proved.

Case: Khadim Hussain vs The State, 2026 SCMR 217. Supreme Court of Pakistan, decided 21 August 2025. Bench: Muhammad Hashim Khan Kakar, Ishtiaq Ibrahim and Ali Baqar Najafi, JJ.
