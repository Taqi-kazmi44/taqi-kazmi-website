2026 SCMR 576: No Dismissal Without a Regular Inquiry

Supreme Court of Pakistan (two-member bench: Justice Ayesha A. Malik and Justice Aqeel Ahmed Abbasi) held in Pakistan Railways vs Syed Qaiser Abbas, 2026 SCMR 576, decided 18 November 2025, that a major penalty like dismissal cannot be awarded without a regular inquiry and an opportunity of being heard; Article 10-A's fair trial guarantee must be read into every service statute. The Service Tribunal's conversion of dismissal into compulsory retirement was left undisturbed.

The Supreme Court of Pakistan has ruled in 2026 SCMR 576 that a major penalty of dismissal from service cannot be awarded without a regular inquiry or an opportunity of being heard. The two-member bench of Justice Ayesha A. Malik and Justice Aqeel Ahmed Abbasi decided The Senior General Manager (CEO), Pakistan Railways vs Syed Qaiser Abbas (Civil Petition No. 2761 of 2023) on 18 November 2025.

The Court held that a regular inquiry is required before awarding any major or minor penalty. The inquiry may be dispensed with only for sufficient reasons disclosed in writing. The fair trial and due process guarantee of Article 10-A of the Constitution must be read into every service statute: the right to be heard is a constitutional right, not a departmental favour.

In this case, the Service Tribunal had already converted the respondent's dismissal into compulsory retirement, and the Supreme Court left that order undisturbed.
