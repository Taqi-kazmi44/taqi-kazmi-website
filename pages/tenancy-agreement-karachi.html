Rent Agreement Karachi: Tenancy Agreement Format (Kiraya Nama)

Kiraya nama mein zaroori shartein

Ek mazboot tenancy agreement (kiraya nama) mein ye baatein wazeh honi chahiye: mahana kiraya aur adaigi ki tareekh, advance ya security deposit ki raqam, kiraye ki muddat, salana izafe ka tareeqa, bijli gas pani ke bil aur maintenance ki zimmedari, notice period, aur muddat khatm hone par jaga khali karane ka tareeqa. Ye shartein baad mein hone wale jhagron se bachati hain.

Tehriri agreement aur tenant verification

Sirf zubani baat par makan ya dukaan kiraye par dena khatarnak ho sakta hai. Tehriri kiraya nama dono fareeqon ke huqooq mehfooz rakhta hai. Sindh mein makan malik ke liye tenant ki police verification karana bhi zaroori samjha jata hai, jiske liye Sindh Police ka tenant registration system maujood hai.

Commercial rent agreements

Dukaan, office, warehouse ya showroom ke commercial kiraya name mein mazeed shartein shamil hoti hain jaise karobar ki noiyat, sign board ki ijazat, sublet ki mumaniat aur renovation ke usool. Commercial agreements ki drafting mein khaas ehtiyat ki zaroorat hoti hai.

DHA, Clifton, Saddar, Gulshan aur North Nazimabad ke liye

DHA Karachi (Phase 1 se 8), Clifton, Saddar, Gulshan-e-Iqbal, North Nazimabad, PECHS aur Bahria Town Karachi mein waqe makan, flat aur dukaanon ke liye kiraya nama drafting ki sahulat faraham ki jati hai. Draft aap ke WhatsApp ya email par bheja jata hai.

Aksar pooche jane wale sawalat

Kya baghair agreement ke kiraya dena qanooni hai?
Tehriri agreement ke baghair huqooq sabit karna mushkil ho jata hai. Hamesha tehriri kiraya nama banwayein.

Kiraya nama kis stamp paper par banta hai?
Kiraya nama aam tor par stamp paper par banaya jata hai. Raqam aur tareeqa maujooda qawaneen ke mutabiq tay hota hai.

Kya purane kiraya nama ki tajdeed ho sakti hai?
Ji haan, muddat khatm hone par nayi shartein ke sath renewal agreement banaya ja sakta hai.
