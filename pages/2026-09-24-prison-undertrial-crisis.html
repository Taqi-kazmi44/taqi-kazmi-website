Pakistan ki jailon mein 73% under-trial: Prison Data Report 2024

Pakistan ki jailon mein har 4 mein se 3 qaidi aise hain jin par abhi koi jurm sabit nahi hua. 102,026 qaidi, 152.2% overcrowding, aur Karachi Central Jail mein 354.92% overcrowding.

Pakistan ki jailon mein har 4 mein se 3 qaidi aise hain jin par abhi koi jurm sabit nahi hua. Ye adaalat ki nakami nahi to aur kya hai?

QANOON KYA KEHTA HAI: Justice Project Pakistan ki Prison Data Report 2024 ke mutabiq Pakistan ki 128 jailon (AJK aur GB samet) mein 102,026 qaidi hain, jo official gunjaish se 152.2% zyada hai. In mein se 73.41% under-trial hain, yani un ka trial abhi mukammal nahi hua aur wo baghair saza ke qaid hain. 2017 mein ye sharah 66% thi, ab barh kar 73.41% ho gayi hai, jab ke aalmi ausat sirf 27% hai. Karachi Central Jail mein 2,400 ki gunjaish ke muqable mein 8,518 qaidi hain, yani 354.92% overcrowding. Saza yafta qaidiyon ki tadaad 3,646 death-row par hai, halan ke December 2019 se koi phansi nahi hui.

MASLA KAHAN HAI: Justice Mansoor Ali Shah ke hawale se Dawn ki report ke mutabiq Pakistan ki adaalaton mein 2.2 million se zyada cases pending hain, jin mein se taqreeban 55,941 sirf Supreme Court mein hain. Trial mein saalon lagne ka matlab hai ke gharib mulzim baghair saza ke apni zindagi, rozgar aur khandan se door saal guzar deta hai. Speedy trial har mulzim ka bunyadi haq hai.

Hawala: Justice Project Pakistan, Prison Data Report 2024 (Tribune): https://tribune.com.pk/story/2523221/jails-overflowing-at-152-capacity ; pending cases (Dawn, Aug 2025): https://www.dawn.com/news/1931206
