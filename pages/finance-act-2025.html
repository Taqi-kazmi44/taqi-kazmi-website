Finance Act 2025

Your salary, your business, your petrol: this one law touches all three.

Your salary, your business, your petrol: this one law touches all three.

The Finance Act, 2025 (in force from 1 July 2025) revises the salaried tax slabs. Pension and annuity income is now taxable, though at a reduced rate. On cash sales above Rs 200,000, 50% of the business expense is no longer deductible. Purchases from non-NTN holders attract a 10% disallowance. The withholding tax on non-filer cash withdrawals rises from 0.6% to 0.8%. FBR arrests in tax fraud cases above Rs 50 million now require approval from a 3-member committee, and production before a magistrate within 24 hours is mandatory. And petroleum now carries a Climate Support Levy of Rs 2.50 per litre.

Every payslip, every business expense sheet, and every fuel price is connected to this Act.
