FBR Orders Electronic Monitoring of Tea Industry in Pakistan

Every retail pack of tea must now be barcode-scanned and recorded before it leaves the factory. The Federal Board of Revenue has ordered electronic production monitoring for the whole packaged tea industry, with a deadline of 10 December 2026.

According to Dawn, the Federal Board of Revenue has ordered every registered tea manufacturer, blender and packer in Pakistan to install electronic production monitoring systems by 10 December 2026. Under Sales Tax General Order No. 35 of 2026, issued under Section 40C of the Sales Tax Act, 1990, every retail pack must be recorded before it leaves the factory.

What the order requires: barcode scanners and counting sensors on every packing line, recording each pack and transmitting the data to the FBR in real time. A unique, machine-readable barcode on every product, based on brand, blend, weight and packaging type, within 15 days of the order. Product details, including brand, weight, retail price, barcode and photograph, must be registered with an authorised vendor, and any new product must be registered at least seven days before packing.

The order covers black tea, green tea, flavoured tea, tea bags, instant tea, tea extracts and premixes, in every retail format from sachets to bulk packs. Toll and contract packers are covered too. The FBR has named 14 manufacturers and packers, including Tapal Tea and Mezan Tea, and authorised four vendors to supply the monitoring systems: Excellence Delivered (ExD), NRTC, Tollink Pakistan and AJCL.

Why is the FBR doing this? The order is part of the drive to close a sales tax gap of more than Rs3 trillion between potential and actual collection. Electronic monitoring is already mandatory in several other sectors.

The order does not change the sales tax rate on tea or any retail price.

What this means for you: if you buy packaged tea, nothing changes in the price you pay. The change happens behind the scenes, at the factory. For tea manufacturers, blenders and packers, the dates to meet are the 15-day barcode requirement and the 10 December 2026 deadline for the full monitoring system. Packing without a registered barcode after the deadline risks action under the Sales Tax Act.
