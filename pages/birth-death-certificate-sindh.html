Registration ka tareeqa

  1. District Administration citizen-services page kholein.
  2. Relevant local council ya district authority identify karein.
  3. Birth ya death registration ka current prescribed form hasil karein.
  4. Current supporting documents authority se confirm karein.
  5. Late registration ho to additional approval ya order requirement poochein.
  6. Fee, timeline aur collection method current basis par confirm karein.
  7. Receipt aur issued certificate ki copy safe rakhein.
