LHC Rules Child Maintenance Cannot Be Reduced by Parents

The Lahore High Court (Justice Usman Ghani Rashid Cheema) has ruled that child maintenance (nan-nufqa) is an absolute right of minor children and cannot be reduced or waived, even with the mutual consent of the parents.

Justice Usman Ghani Rashid Cheema of the Lahore High Court issued a six-page judgment on 3 October 2026 while deciding a petition filed by Shazia Noreen, a mother with custody of two minor children. The mother had earlier signed an affidavit agreeing to a reduced maintenance amount under a mutual parental agreement fixing the child's monthly maintenance at Rs 30,000.

The court set that agreement aside, accepted the petition, and remanded the case back to the family court to decide the matter afresh after evaluating the father's financial status and capability. The court observed that minor children are not our past, but our present.

Maintenance (nan-nufqa) is an absolute legal right of minor children. It cannot be reduced, waived or surrendered, even with the mutual consent of both parents. Neither the parents nor a guardian has the authority to give up this right, and a family court is not bound by a parental settlement affecting the child's welfare or legal rights.
