Consumer Courts Sindh

Cheated by a shopkeeper? Sold a defective product? Harmed by a doctor's negligence? Most people do not know there is a direct forum for exactly this: the Consumer Court.

The Sindh Consumer Protection Act, 2014 (Sindh Act XVII of 2015) sets up a Consumer Court in every district under Section 27. Section 26 lets you claim damages, and selling a fake product can mean 2 years in prison or a fine of Rs 100,000.

For law students, PLD 2022 Sindh 430 is the landmark holding. The Sindh High Court held that medical services fall within the definition of "services", so a patient can approach the Consumer Court directly. A prior decision of the Healthcare Commission is not required, and the Consumer Court can award damages. Watch the limitation: Section 29 time-bars stale claims, and 2026 SHC KHI 1181 has kept that strictness intact.

For the public: keep your receipts and evidence safe, and file your claim in time.
