Family Law Part 6: Haq Mehr

Haq mehr koi tohfa ya ehsaan nahi hai. Pakistan ke family law mein ye biwi ka enforceable qanooni haq hai, aur September 2026 mein Supreme Court ne ye baat dobara wazeh kar di hai.

FAMILY LAW SERIES | Hissa 6 az 10

Haq mehr koi tohfa ya ehsaan nahi hai. Pakistan ke family law mein ye biwi ka enforceable qanooni haq hai, aur September 2026 mein Supreme Court ne ye baat dobara wazeh kar di hai.

QANOON KYA KEHTA HAI: Haq mehr wo raqam ya jaidad hai jo shohar biwi ko Nikah ke contract ke tehat dene ka paband hai. Prompt mehr (muajjal) demand par ada hota hai. Deferred mehr (muwajjal) talaq ya shohar ki wafat par ada hota hai. Muslim Family Laws Ordinance 1961 ki Section 10 ek ahm default deti hai: jahan Nikahnama mein adaigi ke tareeqe ki tafseel na ho, poora mehr demand par ada shuda tasleem hota hai.

WASOOLI: Biwi Family Court mein mehr ki wasooli ka dawa kar sakti hai, aur decree shohar ki jaidad se execute hoti hai. Jahan jaidad mehr mein likhi gayi lekin muntaqil nahi hui, shohar us ki tay shuda qeemat ka zimmedar rehta hai, ya adaalat us waqt ki market value tay karti hai.

MAUJUDA QANOON: September 2026 mein Supreme Court ne kaha ke mehr biwi ka enforceable haq hai, us ki "qeemat" nahi, aur Nikahnama ke mehr wale columns ko mila kar parha jaye. October 2025 mein Adaalat ne kaha ke khula lene wali aurat shaadi ke waqt ke tamam zewar aur mehr ki ashya ki haqdar hai. Adaaltein ab dissolution ke cases mein biwi ka mehr khamoshi se khatam nahi kar saktin.

AAM GHALTI: Dikhawa ke liye ghair haqiqi bara deferred mehr likhwana, ya mehr ka column mubham chhor dena. Mubham column ka matlab Section 10 ke tehat poora mehr demand par ada. Raqam aur tareeqa tehreer mein tay karwayein.

Hawala: Muslim Family Laws Ordinance 1961, Section 10: https://www.sja.gos.pk/assets/Updated_Laws/The_Muslim_Family_Laws_Ordinance_1961.pdf | SC mehr faisla (Tribune): https://tribune.com.pk/story/2630802/supreme-court-rules-dower-is-a-wifes-enforceable-right-not-her-price
