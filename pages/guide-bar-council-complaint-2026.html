How to Complain Against a Lawyer in Pakistan: Bar Council Guide

Your lawyer took the fee and then stopped answering your calls? You do not have to stay silent. The law gives you a forum to complain against a lawyer, and it is the Bar Council, not the police or the court where your case is pending. Here is how it works, step by step.

Step by step

  1. Find out where your lawyer is enrolled. A Supreme Court advocate is reported to the Pakistan Bar Council. Every other advocate is reported to the Provincial Bar Council of that province, or the Islamabad Bar Council.
  2. Write your complaint to the Chairman of the right Bar Council. State each charge clearly, in date order: when you hired the lawyer, what you paid, what was promised, and what was done or not done.
  3. Attach your proof. Fee receipts, bank transfer slips, the wakalatnama, messages, and the order sheets of your case.
  4. Support the complaint with an affidavit on the facts, attested by an oath commissioner. The Bar Council can waive the affidavit.
  5. Pay the prescribed fee, attach the receipt, and file the complaint. Keep a stamped copy for your record. In Punjab, you can also file online through the Punjab Bar Council portal.
  6. The Disciplinary Committee of the Bar Council examines the complaint. It can reject it, ask the lawyer for a reply, or refer it to a Tribunal. A complaint made by the Supreme Court or a High Court cannot be rejected outright.
  7. Before the Tribunal, the case is conducted by the Advocate General of the province. Both sides are heard and evidence is taken. If misconduct is proved, the lawyer can be reprimanded, suspended, removed from practice, or ordered to pay compensation, a fine or a penalty. In a grave case, the lawyer can be suspended at once for up to three months while the Tribunal decides.
  8. If you disagree with the Tribunal's order, you can appeal to the Pakistan Bar Council within 60 days of the order reaching you.

What this means for you

A lawyer is an officer of the court, but he is also accountable. A written complaint with proof is the proper way to hold him to account, and the Bar Council is the forum the law created for exactly this.

Reference
