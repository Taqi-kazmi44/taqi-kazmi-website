LHC Multan ne 24 afraad ki MPO detention ke khilaf writ admit kar li

DC ke ek order par 30 din ki detention? Ab High Court ne mudakhlat kar li. Lahore High Court, Multan Bench ne Sahiwal ke 24 PTI leaders aur workers ki detention ke khilaf writ petition admit kar li hai.

DC ke ek order par 30 din ki detention? Ab High Court ne mudakhlat kar li.

Lahore High Court, Multan Bench ne Sahiwal ke 24 PTI leaders aur workers ki detention ke khilaf writ petition admit kar li hai. Inhein Maintenance of Public Order (MPO) Ordinance ke Section 3 ke tehat 30 din ke liye detain kiya gaya tha.

Qanooni pehlu:

1. MPO Section 3 DC ko preventive detention ka ikhtiyar deta hai, lekin ye ikhtiyar adalati jaanch se bahar nahi.
2. Writ Article 199 ke tehat High Court detention ki qanuni haisiyat check kar sakti hai.
3. Court ne DC Sahiwal se report talab kar li hai.

Akhri faisla aana baqi hai, lekin ye case preventive detention ke qanoon ko samajhne ke liye ahem hai.

Kya aap ke khayal mein MPO ka istemal durust hota hai? Comment mein batayein.
