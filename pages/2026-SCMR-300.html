Your appointment letter was never denied, yet your salary was stopped. What should a court do?

Your appointment letter was never denied, yet your salary was stopped. What should a court do?
The Supreme Court of Pakistan answered this question in Arab v. Province of Sindh.

In 2026 SCMR 300, the Court dealt with petitioners appointed as Driver, Chowkidar and Ward Boy in the Health Department, Tharparkar. The High Court had dismissed their writ petitions, holding that disputed questions of fact could not be resolved under Article 199 of the Constitution.

The Supreme Court held that this dismissal was wrong. The department had never denied or controverted the petitioners' unconditional appointment letters. Instead of dismissing the petitions outright, the High Court could have verified those letters through the department.

The Court converted the petitions into appeals and allowed them. The matter was remanded to the Secretary Health, Government of Sindh, to verify the appointment letters within two months and, if the appointments were on the recommendation of the Departmental Recruitment Committee, to release the due salaries and allow the petitioners to resume their duties.

What this means for you: an undenied appointment letter cannot simply be ignored, and a court should verify the record before closing the door on an employee claim.

Case: Arab v. Province of Sindh through Secretary Health Department, 2026 SCMR 300. Supreme Court of Pakistan, decided 4 June 2025. Bench: Muhammad Ali Mazhar, Syed Hasan Azhar Rizvi and Aqeel Ahmed Abbasi, JJ. Civil Petitions Nos. 933-K to 935-K of 2023.
