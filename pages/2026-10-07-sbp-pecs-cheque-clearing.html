SBP Launches PECS: Image-Based Cheque Clearing from 15 Oct 2026

The State Bank of Pakistan (DISD Circular, 5 Oct 2026) is launching the Pakistan Express Clearing System (PECS): cheques cleared on the basis of captured images instead of physical instruments, under the cheque-truncation concept in Section 2(zl) of the Payment Systems and Electronic Fund Transfers Act, 2007. Pilot from 15 Oct to 15 Nov 2026; nationwide coverage by 30 Apr 2027.

The State Bank of Pakistan has decided to introduce the Pakistan Express Clearing System (PECS) to strengthen the digital payments ecosystem and make cheque clearing faster and more efficient. In a circular issued on 5 October 2026 by its Digital Innovation and Settlements Department, the central bank announced that cheques will now be cleared on the basis of captured images instead of physical instruments, using the concept of cheque truncation defined in Section 2(zl) of the Payment Systems and Electronic Fund Transfers Act, 2007.

Implementation starts on 15 October 2026 on a pilot basis with selected clearinghouse participants, extends to all participants from 16 November to 31 December 2026, and completes with nationwide coverage by 30 April 2027. The presenting or collecting bank captures the cheque image and transmits it to the clearinghouse (NIFT) for onward transmission to the paying or drawing bank. Financial institutions must train staff on truncation, comply with the PS&EFT Act 2007 and the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881, and create public awareness about PECS. Official source: https://www.sbp.org.pk/circulars/disd-circular-no-2-of-2026
