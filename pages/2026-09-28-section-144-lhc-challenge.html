Section 144 Across Punjab Challenged in LHC

Opposition MPAs petition the Lahore High Court against the province-wide 13-day Section 144 CrPC restrictions, questioning their timing, scope and effect on fundamental rights.

Section 144 has been imposed across the whole of Punjab for 13 days. Opposition MPAs have now challenged it before the Lahore High Court.

Punjab Assembly opposition members Ejaz Shafi, Sheikh Imtiaz Mahmood and others, through Advocate Azhar Siddique, have filed a joint petition in the Lahore High Court challenging the restrictions imposed under Section 144 CrPC across the province.

Key points from the petition:
- It questions the timing and legal justification for imposing Section 144 across Punjab only days before the announced protest, which is now postponed.
- It contends that restrictions on peaceful assembly and political activities conflict with fundamental constitutional rights.
- It asks the court to examine the constitutional scope of the government's power to impose Section 144 and the legal basis for any suspension of mobile phone and internet services.
- Through an interim application, it seeks suspension of restrictions on peaceful and unarmed gatherings, and a restraint on arrests, detentions or FIRs against individuals merely for peaceful political activities.

Aam aadmi ke liye iska matlab: Section 144 awami jama hone par pabandi lagata hai, lekin qanoon mein bhi ye sawal maujood hai ke is ka istemal kab aur kahan jayez hai. Adalat is sawal ka jawab degi.
