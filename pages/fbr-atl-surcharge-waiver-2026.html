FBR Draft: Late Filers Can Skip the Rs25,000 ATL Surcharge With a 6-Month Property Freeze

The Federal Board of Revenue has issued draft amendments to the Income Tax Rules, 2002 through S.R.O. 1691(I)/2026 on 6 October 2026. Under the proposal, an individual who files the latest tax year's return after the due date (or extended due date) can be included in the Active Taxpayers' List without paying the Rs25,000 surcharge, if he submits an undertaking in the new Form ATL-U (to be added as Part XV of the First Schedule) through IRIS, agreeing not to buy any property for six months.

What the draft proposes

  1. The waiver: a late filer who submits the Form ATL-U undertaking through IRIS can be included in the ATL without paying the Rs25,000 surcharge.
  2. The undertaking: for six months from the date shown on the IRIS electronic acknowledgement of Form ATL-U, the taxpayer shall not purchase, acquire or otherwise obtain ownership or beneficial interest in any property.
  3. Verification: FBR may check compliance using information from property registries, government agencies, financial institutions and other lawful sources. If a breach is found, the taxpayer first gets an electronic opportunity to explain; if the breach is established, the surcharge-free relief stands withdrawn from the date of the breach, and the taxpayer's ATL status is then governed by Rule 81B(2A), the existing rule for late filers.
  4. Not law yet: this is still a DRAFT. FBR has given stakeholders seven days from 6 October to submit objections or suggestions before it is finalised.
  5. Alongside: FBR also issued S.R.O. 1690(I)/2026 (procedure for electronic scrutiny and intimation of issues detected by the automated system) and S.R.O. 1689(I)/2026 (income tax return form for non-resident ship owners, charterers or authorised shipping agents).

The key point for the common man

  1. Aam aadmi ke liye iska matlab: agar aap ne income tax return late file kiya hai, to Rs25,000 surcharge diye baghair ATL mein shamil hone ka rasta mil sakta hai, lekin 6 mahine tak koi property khareed nahi sakte. Jo is se faida lena chahe, usay IRIS par Form ATL-U submit karna hoga.
  2. Kyunki ye abhi sirf draft hai, faisla karne se pehle final notification ka intezar karein aur apne record mein IRIS acknowledgement ki date zaroor mehfooz rakhein.
