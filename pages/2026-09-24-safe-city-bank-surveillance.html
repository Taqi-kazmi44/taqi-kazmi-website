Karachi Safe City ka data private banks ke liye: Sessions Court ne rok laga di

Aapki gari ki location bank ko kaun de raha tha? Ab court ne rok laga di. Karachi Sessions Court (South) ne order diya hai ke police apna Safe City surveillance network kisi private bank ya financial institution ko defaulter ki gariyan trace karne ke liye istemal nahi kar sakti.

Karachi Sessions Court (South) ne order diya hai ke police apna Safe City surveillance network kisi private bank ya financial institution ko defaulter ki gariyan trace karne ke liye istemal nahi kar sakti.

Key points (AD&SJ Irshad Hussain, order dated 23-09-2026):

1. Koi bhi surveillance, tracking ya location data bank ko sirf tab diya jayega jab qanoon ya competent authority ki ijazat ho.
2. Bank ka koi focal person police ya Safe City system ka independent access nahi le sakta.
3. Police ko 15 din mein compliance report deni hogi ke kis qanoon ke tehat ye madad di ja rahi thi.
4. Bankon ka qanooni recovery ka haq barqarar hai, lekin public resources ka istemal nahi hoga.

Privacy aur property ke haq ka ye ek ahem faisla hai.
