Partnership Registration in Pakistan: Deed & Registrar of Firms

Partnership Act 1932, Sindh amendment ke tehat Registrar of Firms ke paas firm registration.

Aksar pooche jane wale sawalat

Partnership firm ki registration kahan hoti hai?
Partnership Act 1932 ke tehat firm ki registration Registrar of Firms ke daftar mein hoti hai. Sindh mein ye process Sindh Business Registration portal ke zariye bhi shuru kiya ja sakta hai.

Partnership deed mein kya shartein honi chahiye?
Firm ka naam, business ki noiyat, har partner ka munafa-hissa (profit sharing ratio), capital contribution, aur ikhtilaf ki surat mein hal ki shartein wazeh tor par likhi jani chahiye.

Partnership registration ke liye kaun se documents chahiye?
Stamp paper par partnership deed, tamam partners aur do gawahon ke CNIC copies, prescribed form aur paid challan darkar hote hain.

Kya partnership firm ki registration lazmi hai?
Registration lazmi nahi hai, lekin Partnership Act 1932 ki Section 69 ke tehat unregistered firm kuch huqooq, jaise teesre fareeq ke khilaf dawa, istemal nahi kar sakti. Is liye registration karana behtar hai.
