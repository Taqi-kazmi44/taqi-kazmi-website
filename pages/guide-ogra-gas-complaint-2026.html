Wrong Gas Bill? How to File a Gas Complaint with OGRA in Pakistan

Got a wrong gas bill, a faulty meter or low pressure, and the gas company is not fixing it? You do not have to accept it. Here is how to take your complaint to OGRA, the Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority, step by step.

Step by step

  1. Complain to your gas company first. Lodge the complaint with SNGPL (helpline 1199) or SSGC (helpline 1199), online or at a customer service centre, and keep the complaint reference number.
  2. Collect your evidence. Keep the disputed bill, your CNIC, a photo of the meter reading, and previous bills showing your normal usage.
  3. If the company does not give you the redress you asked for, you can escalate to OGRA. OGRA will not accept the complaint until you have first sought redress from the company.
  4. File with OGRA within 90 days of failing to get redress from the company. Apply online at the OGRA complaint portal, or submit a written application in English or Urdu, verified by an affidavit, addressed to the Registrar, OGRA.
  5. Attach your documents. Include your CNIC, the gas bill, the gas supply application if you have it, and the company's reply or review committee decision if any.
  6. OGRA assesses your complaint within 10 days of receiving it, and then asks the gas company for its response.

What this means for you

A wrong gas bill is not a bill you must silently pay, the law gives you a clear escalation route to the regulator.

Reference

Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority Ordinance, 2002 (XVII of 2002), section 42; Complaint Resolution Procedure Regulations, 2003 (S.R.O. 867(I)/2003), Regulations 3, 4, 5 and 6. Official text: ogra.org.pk/download/219.
