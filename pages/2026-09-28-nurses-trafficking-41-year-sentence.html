41-Year Sentence for Trafficking 92 Nurses Abroad

Rawalpindi special court awards 41 years total concurrent imprisonment to the agent who sent 92 nurses to Saudi Arabia on fake experience certificates, plus Rs9.2 million compensation to the victims.

Ninety-two nurses were sent to Saudi Arabia on fake experience certificates and deported within a month. The agent behind it has now been sentenced to 41 years in prison.

In a 2024 case prosecuted by the FIA Islamabad zone, the Judge of the Special Court (Central), Rawalpindi, Abdul Rehman Muhammad Arif, pronounced judgment on September 23, 2026, under Sections 34/420/468/471/109 PPC, Sections 18/22 of the Emigration Ordinance 1979 and Section 5(2) of the Prevention of Corruption Act 1947.

Key points:
- Key agent Ghulam Zulfiqar Ali alias Mian Zulfiqar was awarded 41 years total concurrent rigorous imprisonment plus fines of over Rs9 million.
- The court ordered Rs9.2 million in compensation to the 92 nurses (Rs100,000 each) under Section 544-A CrPC, recoverable as arrears of land revenue, with six months simple imprisonment in default.
- Accomplice Ghulam Fareed received three years RI and a Rs200,000 fine under Section 18 of the Emigration Ordinance, but was acquitted of the remaining charges by giving him the benefit of the doubt.

Aam aadmi ke liye iska matlab: jo agent jaali documents par aap ko bahar bhejta hai, qanoon usay sakht saza deta hai, aur mutasira logon ko muaawza bhi dilaya jata hai. Bahar jaane se pehle apne agent ki verification zaroor karayein.
