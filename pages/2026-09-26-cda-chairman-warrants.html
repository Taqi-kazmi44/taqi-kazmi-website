Bailable arrest warrants issued for CDA chairman over 28-year-old decree

An Islamabad civil court issued bailable arrest warrants for the CDA chairman for failing to implement a court decree for nearly 28 years, after he ignored notices on three consecutive occasions.

Civil Judge Naseerud Din issued bailable arrest warrants for the chairman of the Capital Development Authority over failure to implement a court decree concerning affected residents who had been denied benefits under the authority's rehabilitation policy for nearly three decades.

Counsel for the affected parties submitted that although the CDA had acquired the petitioners' houses, they had not received the promised rehabilitation benefits even after 28 years, and the decree had still not been fully implemented. The chairman had failed to appear before the court despite being served notices on three consecutive occasions. The court directed the relevant authorities to ensure implementation of the decree and warned that failure to comply could lead to further legal action.
