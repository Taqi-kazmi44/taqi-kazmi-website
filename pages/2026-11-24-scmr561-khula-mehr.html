Mst. R. Y. v. S. A., 2026 SCMR 561: In Khula, a Wife in Punjab Returns at Most 25 Percent of Her Prompt Dower

The Supreme Court of Pakistan held that in khula cases in Punjab, a wife returns at most 25 percent of her admitted prompt dower, or up to 50 percent of her deferred dower, under Section 10(5) of the Family Courts Act, 1964.

The nikah fixed prompt dower at 15 tolas of gold, but rukhsati never took place. The wife sought khula. The Family Court decreed khula and ordered her to return 25 percent of the prompt dower, 3.75 tolas. The Appellate Court agreed. The Lahore High Court set both judgments aside and ordered the full 15 tolas back.

The Supreme Court set aside the High Court judgment and restored the Appellate Court judgment. Under Section 10(5) of the Family Courts Act, 1964, as applicable in Punjab, a Family Court in khula may direct the wife to surrender up to 50 percent of her deferred dower or up to 25 percent of her admitted prompt dower. The words "up to" make it a ceiling, not a fixed bill. The court has discretion to order less, but it cannot order more.

The Court held that Section 10(5) makes no distinction between consummated and unconsummated marriages, so rukhsati not taking place does not change the limit. Verse 2:237 concerns talaq, not khula, and the earlier precedent the High Court relied on came from Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, where the Punjab provision does not apply.

The Court also held that bridal gifts and presents given by the husband's family vest absolutely in the bride and cannot be reclaimed, because they are neither dower nor the husband's personal property.

What this means for you: if you are facing khula in Punjab, no one can lawfully demand your entire haq mehr back. The most the court can order is a quarter of the admitted prompt dower. This rule is Punjab specific and does not apply in Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Balochistan or Islamabad, where different law governs.

Reference: Mst. R. Y. v. S. A., 2026 SCMR 561, Supreme Court of Pakistan, bench: Shahid Waheed, Musarrat Hilali (author) and Salahuddin Panhwar, JJ, decided 23 January 2026, Civil Petition No. 2159 of 2025.
