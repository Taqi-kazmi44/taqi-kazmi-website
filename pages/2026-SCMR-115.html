2026 SCMR 115

Out of 3.2 kilograms of narcotics, only 1 kilogram was recovered from each accused.

Out of 3.2 kilograms of narcotics, only 1 kilogram was recovered from each accused.
And that 1 kilogram is exactly what got them bail.

In Muhammad Daud vs The State, 2026 SCMR 115, the Supreme Court held that at the bail stage, each accused is answerable only for the quantity recovered from him. A collective recovery cannot be fastened on every accused unless each one's knowledge is proved. On 1 kilogram, the maximum sentence is 7 years, so the prohibitory clause of Section 497 CrPC was not attracted. Bail is the rule, and jail is the exception.

For law students, study the prohibitory clause of Section 497 CrPC, the quantity table in Section 9(c) of the CNSA, and the classical bail jurisprudence. For citizens, the point is practical: in narcotics cases the entire quantity is often dumped on all the accused together, and this judgment protects against that injustice.

Case: Muhammad Daud vs The State, 2026 SCMR 115. Supreme Court of Pakistan, decided 19 June 2025. Bench: Muhammad Hashim Khan Kakar, Aamer Farooq and Ali Baqar Najafi, JJ.
