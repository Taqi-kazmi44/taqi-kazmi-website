Sindh tenant rights: malik zabardasti kirayedar nahi nikal sakta

Section 13, Sind Rented Premises Ordinance 1979 kehta hai: koi kirayedar us ke qabze se sirf is Ordinance ke tehat nikala ja sakta hai. Eviction order sirf Rent Controller de sakta hai.

Kiraye par rehte hain? Ye aik line aap ke bohat kaam ki hai.

Section 13, Sind Rented Premises Ordinance 1979 kehta hai: koi kirayedar us ke qabze se sirf is Ordinance ke tehat nikala ja sakta hai. Matlab makan malik khud faisla kar ke aap ko nahi nikal sakta, use Rent Controller se eviction order lena lazmi hai.

Aam ghalti: log malik ki dhamki ya dabao mein aa kar ghar khaali kar dete hain, halanke qanoon aap ke sath hai. Agar malik zabardasti kare to Rent Controller se ruju karein.
