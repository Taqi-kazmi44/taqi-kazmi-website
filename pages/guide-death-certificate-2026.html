How to Get a Death Certificate in Pakistan

When a loved one passes away, one document unlocks everything that follows: bank accounts, pension, inheritance and property transfer. That document is the death certificate. Here is how to get it in Pakistan, step by step.

Step by step

  1. Get the hospital death slip if the death happened in a hospital, or the burial slip from the graveyard committee.
  2. Go to the Union Council, Municipal Committee or Cantonment Board of the area where the death occurred.
  3. Take the CNIC of the deceased and your own CNIC. Only a close blood relative (son, daughter, spouse, parent or sibling) can apply.
  4. Fill the death registration form at the office and submit the documents.
  5. The Union Council verifies the record and issues the computerized death certificate. The record is then entered into NADRA's system.
  6. Next, get the deceased's CNIC cancelled at NADRA. A blood relative can apply free of cost through the Pak-ID app or at any NADRA Registration Centre, and NADRA issues a Cancellation Certificate. This must be done within 60 days of the death.

Punjab: free registration for up to seven years

In Punjab, under the Birth and Death Registration Rules 2025, registration is free for up to seven years from the date of death, the regular window is now one year, and a court decree is needed only if registration is delayed beyond seven years. In other provinces, confirm the fee and timeline at your Union Council.

Reference

National Database and Registration Authority Ordinance, 2000, Sections 17 and 17-A (CNIC cancellation within 60 days of death; Cancellation Certificate under Section 17(3)). Punjab Birth and Death Registration Rules, 2025. Dawn, 21 May 2025: https://epaper.dawn.com/news/print/21_05_2025_178_006

What this means for you: without a death certificate, banks, pension offices and inheritance matters stay frozen, so register the death early and keep the CNIC cancellation record safe.
