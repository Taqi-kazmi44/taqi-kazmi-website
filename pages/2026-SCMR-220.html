On what basis was the case standing then?

In Sahad vs Mst. Afsheen, 2026 SCMR 220, the empties found at the crime scene did not match the weapon recovered from the accused, which made the recovery doubtful. The eyewitness gave a general statement in which the accused had no specific role at all. The Supreme Court held this to be a case of further inquiry, and granted bail under Section 497(2) CrPC.

For law students, the study points are the effect of forensic mismatch, specific role attribution, and bail jurisprudence. For citizens, the rule is just: when the forensic evidence itself does not connect, no one can be kept in custody.

Case: Sahad vs Mst. Afsheen, 2026 SCMR 220. Supreme Court of Pakistan, decided 18 August 2025. Bench: Amin-ud-Din Khan and Syed Hasan Azhar Rizvi, JJ.
