FIR Ka Font Bara Karo: LHC Ki Punjab Police Ko Hidayat

Aap ki FIR adalat mein parhi hi na ja sake, to insaf kaise milega? LHC Chief Justice Aalia Neelum ne Punjab police ko FIR ka font bara karne ki hidayat di.

Aap ki FIR adalat mein parhi hi na ja sake, to insaf kaise milega?

Lahore High Court ki Chief Justice Aalia Neelum ne Punjab police ko hidayat di hai ke FIR ka font puray province mein uniform aur bara kiya jaye.

Key points:
- Contempt petition ki samaat mein Chief Justice ne kaha: "While police are saving paper, we are losing our eyesight."
- 3 safhon ki FIR ek safhay par sametne se font parhna namumkin ho jata hai.
- IGP Punjab Rao Abdul Kareem adalat mein pesh huay aur bataya ke Punjab IT Board ko font bara karne ke liye letter bhej diya gaya hai.
- Chief Justice ne IGP se steps ki report talab kar li; agli samaat 29 September ko hogi.
- Ek samaat mein SP Abdullah FIR buland awaz mein parh hi na sakay thay; Chief Justice ne handwriting par kaha: "Even the size of the flies is bigger [than this]."

Aam aadmi ke liye iska matlab: FIR aap ke case ki bunyad hai. Agar FIR saaf aur parhne ke qabil na ho, to investigation aur adalat dono mutasir hote hain.
