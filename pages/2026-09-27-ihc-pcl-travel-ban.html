IHC Strikes Down Rule Letting Agencies Block Foreign Travel

Islamabad High Court ne Passport Rules, 2021 ka wo hissa khatam kar diya jo agencies ko baghair qanooni ikhtiyar ke shehriyon ko travel control list mein dalne deta tha.

The Islamabad High Court has struck down a provision of the Passport Rules, 2021 that allowed government agencies to place a citizen's name on the Passport Control List without lawful authority or due process.

Key points:
- Justice Raja Inaam Ameen Minhas declared the second limb of Rule 22(2)(b), Passport Rules 2021, ultra vires the Passports Act, 1974 and Articles 4, 9, 10A and 15 of the Constitution, holding it of no legal effect.
- The petitioner, Moinuddin, was offloaded at Islamabad airport on Dec 18, 2025 while flying to Saudi Arabia for Umrah with a valid passport, visa and confirmed ticket. No reason was communicated to him. His name had been placed on the PCL on Dec 24, 2024 on an NCB-INTERPOL recommendation, forwarded by the FIA's Assistant Director (Immigration Wing), after he completed a three-year UK sentence. No criminal case, inquiry or investigation is pending against him in Pakistan.
- The court rejected "international reputation" and "deterrence" as grounds for the restraint, noting these reasons appear nowhere in the Act or the Rules.

"The refusal of a passport and the prohibition of travel are two separate exercises of power," Justice Minhas observed. "Gravity does not supply jurisdiction," the judgment said.

Aam aadmi ke liye iska matlab ye hai ke sirf purani saza ya kisi agency ki sifarish par aap ka bahar jana nahi roka ja sakta, jab tak qanoon mein iska wazeh ikhtiyar aur munasib procedure na ho.

The court directed that his name be removed from the PCL within 15 days and barred interference with his lawful foreign travel.
