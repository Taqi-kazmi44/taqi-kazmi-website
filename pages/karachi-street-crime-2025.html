Karachi Street Crime 2025

175 street crimes every day in Karachi. These are not just numbers. This is a legal crisis.

175 street crimes every day in Karachi. These are not just numbers. This is a legal crisis.

Official Sindh Police data, reported by Dawn: in 2025 there were 64,323 street crime incidents, 17,706 cases of mobile snatching at gunpoint, 6,683 vehicles hijacked, 70 people killed, and 290 injured. The CPLC's eleven-month count reached 60,700 plus incidents, with more than 80 people killed while resisting.

For law students: snatching at gunpoint can amount to robbery under Section 392 of the PPC, or dacoity under Section 395 where weapons or five or more persons are involved, and the punishment is strict. The problem is not in the law but in enforcement. Refusal to register an FIR violates Section 154 of the CrPC, and recovery below 10 percent is where the system breaks.

For Karachi: after a snatching, call 15 immediately and then get the FIR registered without fail. Without an FIR, no record for recovery even exists.
