Haal hi mein ek viral dawa tha ke doosri shaadi par maa se bachon ki custody khud ba khud cheen li jati hai. Pakistan ki Supreme Court ne 2026 ke faislay mein is ghalat fehmi ko khatam kar diya hai, jo har walidain ko parhna chahiye.

QANOON KYA KEHTA HAI: Guardians and Wards Act 1890 ki Section 17 ke tehat sarparast muqarrar karte waqt adaalat bache ki welfare ko rehnuma banati hai. Welfare sab se aala ghor hai, dono walidain ke technical dawon se upar.

MAUJUDA QANOON: 2026 ke faislay (Justices Mansoor Ali Shah aur Aqeel Ahmed Abbasi) mein Supreme Court ne 7 aur 8 saal ke do bachon ki custody maa ke paas barqarar rakhi. Adaalat ne kaha: bacha walidain ke faislon ka ghair faal wasool kuninda nahi, balkay faael haq-dar hai jis ki awaz suni jani chahiye; maa ki fitri dekhbhal aur jazbati mustaqil mizaji ka koi mutabadil nahi; maa ki mulazmat us ki ehliyat kam nahi karti, ye us ki mazbooti zahir karti hai; aur dono ne doosri shaadi ki thi lekin sirf doosri shaadi ne kuch faisla nahi kiya. Baap ko bachon se mulaqat (visitation) ke huqooq mile.

PROCEDURE: Custody Guardians and Wards Act 1890 ke tehat Guardian Court mein darkhwast de kar mangi jati hai. Adaalat bache ki umar, sehat, taleem, jazbati talluq, har walid ki salahiyat, aur bacha itna bara ho ke raye de sake to us ki pasand dekhti hai, phir custody aur ghair custodial walid ke liye mulaqat ka schedule tay karti hai.

AAM GHALTI: Samajhna ke custody ek khaas umar par khud ba khud baap ko mil jati hai, ya mulazim maa kamzor custodian hoti hai. Qanoon sirf ek sawal poochta hai: is bache ki welfare kis mein hai.

Hawala: Guardians and Wards Act 1890, Section 17. Case report (Geo Fact Check): https://www.geo.tv/latest/620597-fact-check-supreme-court-has-not-ruled-that-mothers-lose-custody-upon-remarriage
