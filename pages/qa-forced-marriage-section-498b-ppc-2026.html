Is Forcing Someone Into Marriage a Crime in Pakistan?

One of the most common questions people ask: can a family force a woman into a marriage she does not want? The answer is yes, it is a crime. Forcing a woman into marriage is a punishable offence under the Pakistan Penal Code, 1860. This page states the rule exactly as the statute does.

What Section 498-B PPC says

Section 498-B (Prohibition of forced marriage) provides that whoever coerces or in any manner whatsoever compels a woman to enter into marriage shall be punished with imprisonment of either description for a term which shall not be less than three years and may extend to seven years, and shall also be liable to a fine of five hundred thousand rupees.

Heavier punishment for a child or a non-Muslim woman

As amended by the Criminal Laws (Amendment) Act, 2017, the punishment is heavier where the victim is a female child or a non-Muslim woman: imprisonment of either description for a term which shall not be less than five years and may extend to ten years, and a fine of up to one million rupees.

Section 310-A: vani, swara and badla-e-sulh

A related offence is covered by Section 310-A of the Pakistan Penal Code. Whoever gives a female in marriage or otherwise compels her to enter into marriage, as badla-e-sulh, wanni or swara or any other custom or practice under any name, in consideration of settling a civil dispute or a criminal liability, shall be punished with imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to seven years but shall not be less than three years, and shall also be liable to a fine of five hundred thousand rupees.

Consent in nikah

Apart from the criminal punishment, a nikah needs the free consent of both parties to be valid. A marriage entered into under pressure does not carry the same standing as one entered into freely.

The key legal point

  1. No one, not even a parent or elder, can lawfully force a woman into a marriage.
  2. What this means for you: if a forced marriage is being arranged, report it to the police. An FIR can be registered under Section 154 of the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898, and the matter can also be taken before the court.
