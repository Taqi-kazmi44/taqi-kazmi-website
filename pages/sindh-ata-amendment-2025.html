Sindh Ata Amendment 2025

Fighting an ATA case in Karachi? The law behind it has changed.

Fighting an ATA case in Karachi? The law behind it has changed.

The Sindh Assembly passed the Anti-Terrorism (Sindh Amendment) Act, 2025: passed on 8 August 2025, assent by the Governor on 20 August, and notified in the Gazette on 2 September 2025. It amends the Anti-Terrorism Act, 1997 for Sindh, including the definition of "Department" (the Home Department, Government of Sindh) and a change to Section 13.

Street crime, target killing, and extortion cases in Karachi run under the ATA. When the procedure changes, both investigation and trial are affected, so every Karachi lawyer and citizen should know about this amendment.

Verify here (official notification, Provincial Assembly of Sindh): https://www.pas.gov.pk/uploads/acts/Sindh_Act_No.XXIII_of_2025.pdf
