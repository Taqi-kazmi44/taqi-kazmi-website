Two Bail Pleas, One Courtroom: SC Grants Bail to PAEC Driver, Rejects Seminary Teacher's Plea

A two-member Supreme Court bench headed by Justice Jamal Khan Mandokhel granted bail to PAEC driver Ayaz Mahmood in a murder case, while in the same sitting rejecting the bail of seminary teacher Zain Ali of Taxila accused of assaulting a student.

What was reported

  1. The Supreme Court granted bail to Ayaz Mahmood, a Pakistan Atomic Energy Commission driver accused in a murder case. The court noted his name was not mentioned in the FIR and his presence at the crime scene had not been established.
  2. The state alleged that after the murder, the driver transported the other accused in an official vehicle and remained in contact with them; the vehicle was taken into custody. Defence counsel Rizwan Abbasi argued the driver had no role in the offence.
  3. The bench found the case against the driver not made out at the bail stage and granted him bail.
  4. In the same sitting, the bench rejected the bail of seminary teacher Zain Ali of Taxila, accused of assaulting a student. Justice Jamal Khan Mandokhel observed that the teacher has been charge-sheeted and must prove his innocence during the trial.

The key legal point

Bail at the pre-arrest or post-arrest stage turns on what the record shows, not on noise or pressure. An accused whose name does not appear in the FIR and whose link to the crime is not established on the record has a strong case for bail. An accused who has been charge-sheeted for a serious charge faces the trial to establish innocence. Aam aadmi ke liye iska matlab: zamanat ka faisla shor ya dabao par nahi, FIR aur saboot par hota hai.
