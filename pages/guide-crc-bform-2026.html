How to Get Your Child's B-Form (CRC) from NADRA, Step by Step

Does your child have a B-Form yet? Without a Child Registration Certificate (CRC), school admission, a passport, and many other official processes can stop before they start. This guide walks you through the exact NADRA procedure, step by step.

The step-by-step procedure

  1. First, register the child's birth with your Union Council, Municipal Committee, or Cantonment Board, and get the computerized birth registration certificate. NADRA cannot issue a CRC without it.
  2. Make sure both parents' CNICs are valid and that the parents' marital status is correctly updated in NADRA's record. Carry the original CNICs of both parents.
  3. Apply at any NADRA Registration Centre, or through the Pak-ID app. Online application through Pak-ID is available for a child up to one year old. For an older child, apply at a NADRA Registration Centre.
  4. Complete biometric verification. One parent's fingerprints are verified. A child under 3 generally does not need to be present, except when the CRC is being made for a passport. From age 3 a photograph is taken, and from age 10 fingerprints and an iris scan are also taken.
  5. Pay the fee. Normal processing costs Rs 50 and takes 7 working days. Executive processing costs Rs 500 and takes 1 working day. Keep your receipt and tracking number.
  6. Review the printed form, attest it where required, and collect the CRC. You can track the application in the Pak-ID app or by sending the tracking ID by SMS to 8400.

Note

A birth certificate and a B-Form are not the same document. If the date of birth on the CRC differs from the Union Council certificate, correct the birth certificate first, and NADRA then updates the CRC to match.

The governing source

The procedure is published by the National Database and Registration Authority (NADRA) for its Child Registration Certificate (CRC) service, on the official portal id.nadra.gov.pk. Fees and timelines can change, so check NADRA's current fee schedule before you pay.

The key legal point

  1. What this means for you: get the Union Council birth certificate first, check that both parents' NADRA records are correct, and the CRC itself is a low cost, one visit process for most families.
