Fir Right 154

"The police won't write my FIR." It is Pakistan's most common complaint, and the solution is already written in the law.

"The police won't write my FIR." It is Pakistan's most common complaint, and the solution is already written in the law.

Section 154 of the CrPC makes registering an FIR in a cognizable offence a duty of the police, not a favour. A refusal is a violation of the law itself.

For law students: Sections 22-A and 22-B of the CrPC provide the remedy. An application is filed before the Justice of Peace (the District and Sessions Judge or the Additional District and Sessions Judge), who can order the registration of the FIR. The superior courts have repeatedly held that registration of an FIR is mandatory on information of a cognizable offence. This is the most-used remedy in criminal procedure, and every practitioner must know how to draft it.

For the public: if the police station refuses, call 1715, the IGP Complaint Cell, and report "FIR not registered" along with your CNIC number. There is also an online portal: igpcms.sindhpolice.gov.pk.

Verify here (official Sindh Police IGP complaint system FAQ): https://igpcms.sindhpolice.gov.pk/faqs
