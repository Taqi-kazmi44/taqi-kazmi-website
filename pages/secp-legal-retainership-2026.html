Legal Adviser Retainership: C.P. D-1766/2024

Big news for young lawyers: companies above the capital limit must now keep an advocate on retainer, and one advocate can serve only three such companies.

The Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan has directed every company with paid-up capital above Rs 20 million to appoint a practising advocate as its legal adviser on retainership, and no advocate can hold more than three such companies at the same time.

The direction enforces Section 10 of the Lawyers Welfare and Protection Act, 2023. Companies must report their legal adviser's details to the Registrar on Form-9 under Section 197 of the Companies Act, 2017, and the SECP has warned of action against companies that do not comply.

The legal push came from the High Court of Sindh at Karachi, where Advocate Jahangir Shams, Sheraz Shams and others petitioned in July 2026 against companies ignoring the retainer law, and notices were issued to the respondents.

On the same law, in C.P. No. D-1766 of 2024, the High Court of Sindh, Circuit Court Hyderabad, heard Advocate Masood Illahi Sahito in person. On 16 December 2025, the Additional Advocate General Bashir Ahmed Almani and the Assistant Advocate General Sindh Rafique Ahmed Dahri stated that the Lawyers Welfare and Protection Act, 2023 shall be implemented in its letter and spirit. The Court observed that the Bar Councils are the statutory and regulatory bodies for the implementation of advocates' welfare law, and disposed of the petition with consent in terms of those statements.

What this means for you: if you are a young advocate, companies now legally need lawyers on retainer, and if you run a company above the capital limit, appointing a legal adviser is no longer optional. Because one advocate can now serve only three companies, retainerships will have to be shared across the profession instead of staying with a few names.

Case: C.P. No. D-1766 of 2024, High Court of Sindh, Circuit Court Hyderabad, order dated 16 December 2025. Official order: caselaw.shc.gov.pk. SECP advisory reported by APP: UrduPoint. Also published on LinkedIn: Taqi Kazmi on LinkedIn.
