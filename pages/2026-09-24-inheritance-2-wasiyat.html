Inheritance Law (2/7): Wasiyat sirf ek tihai tak, waris ke haq mein nahi

Meri tamam jaidad mere ek bete ke naam. Kya aisi wasiyat qanoon ki nazar mein chalegi? Jawab: nahi, Hanafi qanoon mein Musalman sirf ek tihai hissa ghair waris ko wasiyat kar sakta hai.

INHERITANCE LAW SERIES | Hissa 2 az 7

Meri tamam jaidad mere ek bete ke naam. Kya aisi wasiyat qanoon ki nazar mein chalegi? Jawab: nahi, aur is ki wajah samajhna har ghar ke liye zaroori hai.

QANOON KYA KEHTA HAI: Hanafi qanoon mein Musalman apni khalis jaidad ka sirf ek tihai hissa wasiyat ke zariye kisi ghair waris ko de sakta hai. Baqi do tihai lazmi tor par warison mein taqseem hoga. Kisi waris ke haq mein ki gayi wasiyat doosre warison ki ijazat ke baghair invalid hai, chahe wo ek tihai ke andar hi kyun na ho. Ye usool Privy Council ke mashhoor faisle Ghulam Muhammad v. Ghulam Hussain (1932 PC 81) mein tay hua, jo aaj bhi Pakistani adaalaton mein binding precedent hai. Agar wasiyat ek tihai se zyada ki ho to wo us had tak moassar hogi jis ki waris wafat ke baad ijazat dein. Wazeh rahe ke ye ijazat wasi ki zindagi mein nahi, us ki wafat ke baad di jana zaroori hai.

AAM GHALTI: Log samajhte hain ke wasiyat likh dene se waris ko poori tarah mehrum kiya ja sakta hai. Qanoon is ke bilkul bar aks hai: wasiyat ka maqsad ghair waris (jaise koi rishtedar jo waris nahi, ya falaahi kaam) ke liye ek tihai tak gunjaish dena hai, warison ke haq cheenna nahi.

Hawala: Ghulam Muhammad v. Ghulam Hussain, 1932 PC 81 (Privy Council)
