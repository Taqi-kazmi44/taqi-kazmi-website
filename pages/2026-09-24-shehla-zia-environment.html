Shehla Zia vs WAPDA: ek khat jo Pakistan ki tareekh ka ahem faisla bana

Islamabad mein grid station lagne se rokne ke liye likha gaya ek khat Supreme Court ka landmark faisla ban gaya. Court ne wazeh kiya ke Article 9 ke right to life mein saaf aur sehatmand mahol ka haq bhi shaamil hai.

Sirf ek khat ne Supreme Court ko hila diya.

Ek shehri ne Supreme Court ko khat likha aur Islamabad mein lagne wala grid station ruk gaya. Wo khat Pakistan ki tareekh ke sab se ahem faislon mein se ek ban gaya: Shehla Zia vs WAPDA (PLD 1994 SC 693).

Supreme Court ne faisla diya ke Article 9 ke tehet right to life mein saaf aur sehatmand mahol ka haq bhi shaamil hai. Articles 9 aur 14 ko mila kar Court ne kaha: zindagi ka matlab sirf jeena nahi, izzat, sehat aur sukoon ke saath jeena hai. Court ne precautionary principle lagaya: ehtiyat ilaaj se behtar hai, aur scientific uncertainty awam ki sehat ko khatre mein dalne ka jawaz nahi.

Ye faisla Justice Saleem Akhtar ne tehreer kiya aur aaj bhi Pakistan ke environmental law ki bunyaad hai. Pollution, smog aur shehri mansubah bandi ke cases mein aaj bhi isi faisle ka hawala diya jata hai.

Verify karein: https://www.escr-net.org/caselaw/2015/ms-shehla-zia-v-wapda-pld-1994-sc-693/
