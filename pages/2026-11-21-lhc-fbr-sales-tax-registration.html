LHC: FBR Cannot Collect Sales Tax Before Registration in Pakistan

The FBR cannot demand sales tax from you before it registers you. The Lahore High Court has made the sequence clear: registration first, tax collection later.

Registration first, tax collection later

Justice Muhammad Ajmal Zahid held on Friday, 20 November 2026, that Section 3 of the Sales Tax Act, 1990, can only be invoked against a person after his registration. The court said the FBR must first follow the prescribed legal sequence to register a person liable to be registered, and only after registration can it determine and recover tax.

Four tax references dismissed

The court dismissed all four Sales Tax References filed by the Inland Revenue and maintained the Appellate Tribunal Inland Revenue's decision of 15 March 2022. It also declared the department's case on ghee and cooking oil supplies from 2016 to 2019 null and void, directing the department to register first and then collect tax.

What this means for you

If you run a business and the FBR demands sales tax without first registering you, that demand is not lawful. Registration is the first step, not an afterthought, and a taxpayer can rely on this judgment when the department skips it.
