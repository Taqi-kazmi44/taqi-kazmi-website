How to Check Your FBR Active Taxpayer List (ATL) Status by SMS in Pakistan

Are you a filer or not? One SMS gives you the answer in seconds. The Active Taxpayer List is FBR's public list of people and businesses who have filed their income tax return for the relevant tax year, and your place on it decides how much withholding tax you pay. Here is how to check it, step by step.

Step by step

  1. Type ATL, a space, then your 13 digit CNIC without dashes.
  2. Send it to 9966 from any mobile network. The reply tells you whether you are Active on the Active Taxpayer List.
  3. Companies and associations of persons send ATL, a space, then their 7 digit NTN to the same number, 9966.
  4. You can also check online, free and without login. Open fbr.gov.pk, go to Online Services, Active Taxpayer List, enter your CNIC or NTN and the tax year.
  5. If the reply says you are not active, file your income tax return for the relevant tax year on the FBR IRIS portal at iris.fbr.gov.pk. Registration alone does not make you active. Only a filed return does.
  6. If you filed after the due date, a late filer surcharge applies under section 182A: Rs 25,000 for an individual since 1 July 2026. In some cases FBR also accepts an undertaking, Form ATL-U, instead of the surcharge.
  7. Allow a few days after filing or paying for the list to update, then check again.

Why your ATL status matters

Being on the Active Taxpayer List means lower withholding tax on bank transactions, property deals and vehicle transfers. Staying off it costs you extra tax on almost every big transaction.

Reference

Income Tax Ordinance, 2001, section 181A (Active Taxpayers List) and section 182A (late filer surcharge); Income Tax Rules, 2002, rule 81B. Official portals: fbr.gov.pk and iris.fbr.gov.pk.

What this means for you: a filed return is what puts you on the list, and the list is what keeps your tax rates low, so check your status today and file if you are not on it.
