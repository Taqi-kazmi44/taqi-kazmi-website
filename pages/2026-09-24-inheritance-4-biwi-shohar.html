Inheritance Law (4/7): Biwi aur shohar ka virasat mein hissa

Surah An-Nisa (4:12) ke mutabiq bewah ka hissa: aulad ho to 1/8, aulad na ho to 1/4. Shohar ka hissa: aulad ho to 1/4, na ho to 1/2. Ye hissa haq mehr se alag hai.

INHERITANCE LAW SERIES | Hissa 4 az 7

Shohar ke inteqal ke baad biwi ko ghar se nikal dena, ya biwi ke inteqal par shohar ka hissa dabaa lena. Dono suraton mein qanoon ka jawab ek hai, aur wo Surah An-Nisa mein likha hai.

QANOON KYA KEHTA HAI: Surah An-Nisa (4:12) ke mutabiq bewah ka hissa is par munhasir hai ke marhoom ki aulad thi ya nahi. Agar aulad ho (beta ho ya beti) to biwi ko kul jaidad ka aathwan hissa (1/8) milega. Agar aulad na ho to biwi ka hissa chautha (1/4) hoga. Isi tarah agar biwi ka inteqal ho to shohar ko, aulad ki maujoodgi mein chautha hissa (1/4), aur aulad na hone ki surat mein aadha hissa (1/2) milega. Ye hissay qarze aur wasiyat ki adayegi ke baad banti hui khalis jaidad mein se hain.

AHM NUQTA: Biwi ka ye hissa us ke haq mehr se alag hai. Agar shohar ne mehr ada nahi kiya tha to wo mehr marhoom ka qarz hai, jo taqseem se pehle biwi ko ada hoga, aur phir us ke ilawa biwi ko upar bataya gaya virasat ka hissa bhi milega.

AAM GHALTI: Log biwi ke virasat ke hissay ko kharcha ya ehsaan samajhte hain. Ye ehsaan nahi, us ka muqarar qanooni haq hai jo maut ke waqt hi usay muntaqil ho jata hai.

Hawala: Surah An-Nisa (4:12)
