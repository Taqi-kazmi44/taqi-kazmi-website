Inheritance Law (7/7): NADRA succession certificate ka paanch step tareeqa

Walid ke inteqal ke baad bank account ya plot transfer karwana hai? Pehle succession certificate chahiye hoga. Achhi khabar: ab is ke liye saalon adaalat ke chakkar nahi katne parte, NADRA paanch steps mein jari karta hai.

INHERITANCE LAW SERIES | Hissa 7 az 7

Walid ke inteqal ke baad bank account ya plot transfer karwana hai? Pehle succession certificate chahiye hoga. Achhi khabar: ab is ke liye saalon adaalat ke chakkar nahi katne parte.

QANOON KYA KEHTA HAI: Letters of Administration and Succession Certificates Act 2020 (aur Sindh mein Sindh ka 2021 ka Act) ke tehat NADRA apne Succession Facilitation Units ke zariye succession certificate jari karta hai. Tareeqa paanch steps mein hai. Pehle: darkhwast, apna CNIC, marhoom ka death certificate aur affidavit ke sath. Doosre: tamam warison aur jaidad ki tafseel darj hoti hai. Teesre: tamam warison ki biometric verification, jo mulk ke kisi bhi NADRA centre par ho sakti hai. Chauthe: akhbar mein ishtehar de kar 14 din tak awam se aitrazat mange jate hain. Panchwen: agar koi aitraz na aaye to certificate print kar ke de diya jata hai, aam tor par chand hafton mein. August 2025 se ye sahulat mulk ke tamam NADRA centres par dastiyab hai.

AHM SHART: Ye rasta sirf tab hai jab tamam waris mutafiq hon. Agar warison mein ikhtelaf ho, koi waris nabaligh ho, ya koi haqeeqi tanaza ho to NADRA inkar kar dega aur muamla Succession Act 1925 ke tehat adaalat mein jayega. Fee waqt ke sath badalti rehti hai, is liye raqam NADRA se confirm karein.

AAM GHALTI: Ghalat document par apply kar dena. Bank account ke liye succession certificate aur jaidad ke liye letter of administration hota hai. Pehle bank ya registry office se confirm kar lein ke unhein kaun sa document chahiye.

Hawala: Letters of Administration and Succession Certificates Act 2020; NADRA process: https://www.brecorder.com/news/amp/40102309
