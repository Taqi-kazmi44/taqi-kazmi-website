Family Law Part 4: Khula Procedure in Pakistan

Khula aur DMMA 1939 ki statutory dissolution alag remedies hain. Family Courts Act 1964 filing, service, reconciliation, trial aur decree ka framework deta hai.

FAMILY LAW SERIES | Hissa 4 az 10

PROCESS OF KHULA IN PAKISTAN

Khula aur Dissolution of Muslim Marriages Act 1939 ke tehat statutory dissolution ko ek samajhna durust nahi. Sahi legal ground pleading, evidence aur dower ke nateeje ko affect kar sakta hai.

JURISDICTION: West Pakistan Family Courts Act 1964 ki Section 5, Schedule ke sath parhi jaye, Family Court ko dissolution of marriage, including khula, par exclusive jurisdiction deti hai.

PLAINT AUR DOCUMENTS: Section 7 ke mutabiq plaint mein material facts, witnesses ki list aur relied-on documents diye jate hain. Jo document plaintiff ke qabze mein na ho, us ki list aur relevance state ki jati hai.

SUMMONS: Section 8 defendant ki service aur written statement ke process ko govern karti hai.

RECONCILIATION: Section 10 pre-trial proceedings mein compromise ya reconciliation ki koshish ko address karti hai. Agar reconciliation fail ho to Family Court applicable statutory text aur current provincial amendments ke mutabiq case proceed karti hai.

TRIAL AUR DECREE: Section 12 evidence ke baad further reconciliation effort, judgment aur decree se relevant hai. Agar biwi Section 2, Dissolution of Muslim Marriages Act 1939 ki statutory ground plead kare to us ground ka proof relevant hota hai.

SECTION 2 DMMA 1939: Is section mein shohar ka chaar saal se la-pata hona, do saal maintenance na dena, qaid, marital obligations perform na karna, impotence, specified illness, cruelty aur Muslim law ke tehat recognised grounds shamil hain. Exact clause facts ke mutabiq identify hoti hai.

DOWER / HAQ MEHR: Har dissolution ko khula keh dena theek nahi. Khula aur statutory dissolution ke dower consequences mukhtalif ho sakte hain. Court ko pleadings aur evidence ke mutabiq relief decide karna hota hai.

FEES: Is guide mein koi fixed court fee, lawyer fee ya certificate fee quote nahi ki gayi. Current court fee, process cost aur professional fee filing se pehle relevant Family Court aur service provider se written form mein confirm karein.

Exact legal references: Dissolution of Muslim Marriages Act 1939, Section 2. West Pakistan Family Courts Act 1964, Sections 5, 7, 8, 10 and 12, read with the Schedule and current Sindh amendments.
