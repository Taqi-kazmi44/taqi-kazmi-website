Ict Child Marriage 2025

Marriage under 18 is now a crime in Islamabad.

Marriage under 18 is now a crime in Islamabad.

The ICT Child Marriage Restraint Act, 2025 fixes the minimum marriage age at 18 for both boys and girls. The Nikah registrar must verify the CNIC of both parties; violation carries 1 year of imprisonment and a fine of Rs 100,000. An adult who marries a child faces up to 3 years in prison. Living in a marital relationship with a child is declared statutory rape. Parents or guardians who facilitate such a marriage face 2 to 3 years of imprisonment.

Pakistan has nearly 19 million child brides. This is the first federal law to set the 18-year threshold for both, and it is becoming a model for the provinces. Presidential assent came on 29 May 2025.
