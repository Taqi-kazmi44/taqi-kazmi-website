LHC stays auction of local government shops in Jaranwala

The Lahore High Court restrained authorities from auctioning local government shops occupied by 40 Jaranwala petitioners holding valid 2023 lease agreements, and summoned the Faisalabad DC in person on Sept 28.

The Lahore High Court on Thursday restrained the authorities from proceeding with the auction of local government's shops occupied by 40 petitioners in Jaranwala, and summoned the Faisalabad deputy commissioner in person on Sept 28.

A two-judge bench comprising Justice Malik Waqar Haider Awan and Justice Malik Javed Iqbal Wains passed the stay order while hearing an intra-court appeal filed by the shopkeepers against a single-bench judgement. Advocate Shoaib Saleem argued that the appellants held valid written lease agreements executed in 2023 after approval by the district assessment committee, had agreed to revised rents, and the Jaranwala Municipal Corporation had subsequently started receiving rent from them.

The bench asked how the shops could be auctioned when written agreements regarding the appellants' rights were already before the court. The counsel argued that auctioning shops already leased to the appellants was unlawful and contrary to Section 94 of the Punjab Local Government Act, 2022 and the Punjab Local Government (Property) Rules, 2008. The bench stayed the auction and directed the Faisalabad DC to appear in person to explain the government's position. Meanwhile, a single bench comprising Justice Ahmad Nadeem Arshad also stayed auction proceedings against several other Jaranwala shopkeepers in similar petitions.
