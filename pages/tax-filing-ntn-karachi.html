NTN Registration Karachi: Tax Filing Guide Pakistan

FBR se NTN registration, income tax return filing aur sales tax registration ka mukammal guide. Karachi ke individuals, freelancers aur businesses ke liye.

NTN registration ka procedure

  1. FBR IRIS portal par account banayein
  2. CNIC aur business documents upload karein
  3. NTN registration form jama karayein
  4. Verification ke baad NTN jari ho jata hai

Income tax return filing

Har tax year ka income tax return FBR IRIS portal par online file hota hai. Return mein aamdani, akhrajat, assets aur tax deduction ki tafseel darj hoti hai. Waqt par return file na karne par jurmana aur additional tax lag sakta hai.

Sales tax registration

Muqarrara had se zyada turnover wale businesses ke liye sales tax registration lazmi hai. Registration ke baad mahana sales tax returns file karne hote hain.

DHA, Clifton, Saddar aur Gulshan ke liye

DHA Karachi, Clifton, Saddar, Gulshan-e-Iqbal, PECHS aur Bahria Town Karachi mein waqe individuals, freelancers, dukandaron aur companies ke liye NTN registration aur tax filing mein madad faraham ki jati hai.

Aksar pooche jane wale sawalat

Kya salaried person ko bhi NTN chahiye?
Taxable salary par tax kat-ta hai, lekin NTN aur return filing se filer status milta hai jisse withholding tax ki sharah kam ho jati hai.

Filer aur non-filer mein kya farq hai?
Waqt par return file karne wala filer kehlata hai aur kayi transactions par kam tax deta hai. Non-filer par zyada sharah lagti hai.

Kya purane saalon ke returns file ho sakte hain?
Ji, purane tax years ke returns bhi jurmane ke sath file kiye ja sakte hain.
