Legal FAQ

Aam Sawal, Mukhtasar Jawab

Website ki service aur article research se liye gaye jawab. Har matter ke facts mukhtalif ho sakte hain.

Police FIR darj na kare to kya karein?

Cognizable offence mein FIR darj karna police ki duty hai. Existing article ke mutabiq IGP Complaint Cell 1715 par complaint ki ja sakti hai, aur Sections 22-A/22-B CrPC ke remedy ke liye Justice of Peace se ruju hota hai.

Arrest ke baad police kitni dair custody mein rakh sakti hai?

Article 10(2) aur Section 61 CrPC ke mutabiq arrested person ko 24 ghante ke andar magistrate ke samne pesh karna hota hai, lekin arrest ki jagah se magistrate tak safar ka zaroori waqt is muddat mein shamil nahi hota. Mazeed custody ke liye magistrate ka order darkaar hota hai.

NTN registration ke liye bunyadi documents kya hain?

Individual ke liye CNIC, apne naam ka bank certificate, registered SIM aur email. Business ho to premises proof aur recent utility bill bhi darkaar ho sakte hain.

Company incorporation ka pehla qadam kya hai?

SECP eZfile par account aur proposed company name reservation se process shuru hota hai. Form-1 aur promoters ke IDs service detail mein listed hain.

Police character certificate ke documents aur fee kya hain?

Application Sindh Police PRVS portal par hoti hai. Website ko mile retrievable source par mukammal checklist, fee aur processing time confirm nahi ho sake, is liye ye official portal par confirm karein.

Talaq ka qanooni procedure kya hai?

Existing family-law article ke mutabiq Chairman Union Council ko written notice aur biwi ko us ki copy deni hoti hai. Notice milne ke baad statutory procedure aur 90-day period apply hota hai.

Khula aur talaq ek hi cheez hain?

Nahi. Khula biwi ka court-based remedy hai, jabke talaq aur statutory dissolution ke procedures aur maali asraat mukhtalif ho sakte hain.

Haq mehr demand par mil sakta hai?

Prompt mehr demand par ada hota hai. Website ke article ke mutabiq agar Nikahnama payment mode par khamosh ho to Section 10 MFLO ka default relevant hota hai.

NADRA succession route kab use hota hai?

Jab tamam waris mutafiq hon. Tanaza, minor heir ya genuine dispute ki surat mein matter adaalat ja sakta hai. Fee NADRA se current basis par confirm karein.

Lawyer choose karte waqt kya verify karein?

Current bar enrollment, relevant practice area, relevant jurisdiction, written scope aur fee arrangement verify karein. Taqi Kazmi LLB student hain; website legal information, documentation aur registration assistance deti hai, court representation ka claim nahi karti.

Process of khula in Pakistan kya hai?

Family Court mein dissolution suit file hota hai. Family Courts Act 1964 ke Section 5 read with Schedule jurisdiction deta hai; Sections 7 aur 8 plaint aur summons, Section 10 reconciliation, aur Section 12 judgment aur decree se relevant hain. DMMA 1939 ki Section 2 statutory grounds ko khula se alag rakhein.

NADRA succession certificate fee aur online verification kahan milegi?

Current fee, eligibility, online application aur verification sirf official NADRA succession portal par confirm karein. Website unverified amount quote nahi karti.

Kya assistance pure Pakistan mein available hai?

Federal registrations aur online documentation guidance Pakistan bhar ke clients ke liye available hai. Provincial requirements relevant authority ke mutabiq confirm hoti hain.
