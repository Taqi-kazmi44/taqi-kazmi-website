Legal Glossary

Qanooni Istilahat Ki Wazeh Tashreeh

Police, court aur documents ke process mein baar baar samne aane wali common terms.

FIR
Cognizable offence ki police ke paas pehli formal information.
Challan
Investigation ke baad police ki court mein jama report.
Bail
Trial ya inquiry ke dauran sharaait ke sath custody se rihaai.
Remand
Magistrate ki authority se mazeed custody ka qanooni order.
Affidavit
Qasam par diya gaya written statement.
Deed
Haq ya transaction ko record karne wala formal document.
Petition
Court se relief mangne ki formal darkhwast.
Summons
Court ki taraf se hazri ka formal notice.
Decree
Civil ya family case mein court ka formal final determination.
NTN
FBR tax registration ka National Tax Number.
Khula
Biwi ki taraf se Family Court ke zariye nikah khatam karne ka remedy.
Succession certificate
Marhoom ke movable assets ke liye warison ki authority ka document.
