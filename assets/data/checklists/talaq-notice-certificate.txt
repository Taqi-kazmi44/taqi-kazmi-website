TALAQ NOTICE / CERTIFICATE
Printable statutory checklist

[ ] Talaq pronounce hone ke baad Chairman Union Council ko written notice dein
[ ] Notice ki copy wife ko supply karein
[ ] Delivery proof aur submitted copy sambhal kar rakhein
[ ] Arbitration Council ki communication ka record rakhein
[ ] Statutory 90-day period complete hone se pehle final effectiveness assume na karein
[ ] Certificate issuance ka current Union Council process confirm karein
