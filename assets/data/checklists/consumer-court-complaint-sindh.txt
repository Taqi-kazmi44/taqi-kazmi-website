CONSUMER COURT COMPLAINT, SINDH
Printable official-source checklist

[ ] Defect, loss ya faulty service ka written chronology tayyar karein
[ ] Official manual ke mutabiq Consumer Court claim se pehle 15-day written notice requirement review karein
[ ] Jurisdiction, limitation aur relief claimed verify karein
[ ] Complaint aur supporting copies filing se pehle professional review karwayein
