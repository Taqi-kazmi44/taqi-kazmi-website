CNIC NEW / RENEWAL
Printable official-process checklist

Fresh/new CNIC:
[ ] Nearest NADRA Registration Centre ka route confirm karein
[ ] Token hasil karein
[ ] Photograph, fingerprints aur signature capture complete karein
[ ] Printed application form ki tamam entries review karein
[ ] Attestation ya eligible blood relative biometric path official staff se confirm karein

Renewal:
[ ] NADRA Registration Centre ya Pak Identity Mobile App ka eligible route use karein
[ ] Current fee aur category official channel par confirm karein

Official NADRA portal: https://id.nadra.gov.pk/

NADRA ke mutabiq fresh/new CNIC website se apply nahi hota. Current supporting documents official channel par verify karein.
